# Savage StarCraft — Design Charter v0.1

**Status:** Working foundational charter (agreed project direction; individual mechanics remain unapproved).

## Mission
Produce a lore-faithful, playable adaptation of the StarCraft universe for Savage Worlds Adventure Edition. Provide reusable player options, equipment, creature profiles, vehicle profiles, game-master tools, and campaign material.

## Design principles
1. **Fiction over RTS balance.** Treat missions, dialogue, manuals, cinematics, novels, and other story material as evidence; do not directly convert RTS hit points, weapon damage, attack ranges, population/supply values, or visual scale.
2. **SWADE first.** Use core mechanics and relevant Companion material whenever feasible. Write bespoke rules only for setting-defining effects not otherwise represented satisfactorily.
3. **Toolbox before archetype.** Design ancestry, skills, Edges, powers, gear, and training as reusable components. Marine, Firebat, Marauder, Medic, Reaper, Ghost, etc., can be sample builds or GM profiles; they are not restrictive character classes.
4. **Separate equipment from wielder.** Equipment has consistent statistics and can be carried, traded, lost, modified, or operated when physiology, training, and logistics permit. A Marine is not universally locked to a C-14 gauss rifle.
5. **Fictional asymmetry is permitted.** A Zergling, a Marine, a Zealot, an Ultralisk, and a battlecruiser need not have comparable power budgets. Balance playable characters in their campaign context; do not artificially equalize all adversaries.
6. **Honor scale and tactical roles.** The distinction between small arms, AP/Heavy Weapon threats, Heavy Armor, walkers, colossal organisms, and warships should be meaningful. Twenty rifles cannot defeat a capital ship just because they can in the RTS.
7. **Fast, Furious, Fun.** Avoid elaborate damage, ammunition, technology, and evolution simulation unless play requires it. Use SWADE Dramatic Tasks, Quick Encounters, Chases, Clashes, and Mass Battles where suitable.
8. **Lore research is auditable.** Distinguish explicit canon, in-universe belief, reasonable extrapolation, estimated scale, fan assertion, and original adaptation choices. Document source/claim provenance.
9. **Player-facing flexibility.** Allow military, frontier, exploration, espionage, mercenary, Protoss-focused, horror, and other viable campaigns; do not assume one permanent party or one era.
10. **Playtest before cataloguing everything.** Complete a small end-to-end demonstration, then expand verified mechanics across the universe.

## Construction layers
- **Character capabilities:** ancestry, skills, Edges, Hindrances, psionics, cybernetics and backgrounds.
- **Tools and technology:** personal weapons, armor, specialist gear, consumables, vehicles, installations.
- **Ready-to-run profiles:** military roles, alien organisms, Named Wild Cards, and ships assembled or built as appropriate. NPCs need not obey PC build point budgets.

## Scale benchmark
Test at least these interactions before broad equipment release:
- Unarmored civilian vs. powered infantry rifle.
- Armored Marine vs. standard and anti-armor arms.
- Firebat/Marauder vs. light and heavy Zerg creatures.
- Infantry squad vs. Ultralisk (primarily tactical avoidance, special weapons, support).
- Infantry vs. battlecruiser (not a credible small-arms target).
- Vehicles/aircraft vs. each other under the appropriate SWADE subsystem.

## SWADE sources
- Core Rules (Adventure Edition), especially Gear, Powers, Combat, Vehicles, and Mass Battles.
- Science Fiction Companion: heroes, power armor, robots, vehicles, walkers, starships, powers, setting rules.
- Vehicle Guide: physical size/target profile/volume vs mass; conversions are starting points, not absolute formulae.
- World Builder & Game Master's Guide, especially World Building and Converted Settings (printed pp. 3–11): focus on core themes, existing Edges/skills, and a small number of necessary Setting Rules.
- Fantasy, Horror, and Super Powers Companions: optional targeted references only when justified by a specific requirement; not blanket imports.

## Approval gates
A mechanic is not complete unless it has (a) cited fictional behavior, (b) clearly specified SWADE implementation, (c) comparison to SWADE precedents, (d) clear threat/scale assumptions, and (e) at least a documented test or review appropriate to its impact.

## Scope not yet decided
Exact campaign start era, mixed Terran/Protoss PC balance, playable Zerg variants, method for shields, psionics, combined-arms threat scaling, and starship-combat granularity remain explicit design questions. No decision is implied merely by listing a possible subsystem.
