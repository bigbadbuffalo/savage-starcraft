# Development Roadmap

**State:** Phase 1 started; the rest is planned, not already completed. Source and mechanical research advance iteratively.

| Phase | Objective | Completion gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Foundations | Approve charter, canon policy, research protocol, registry, initial continuity disputes | A documented process, at least two test cases, and explicit unresolved questions |
| 2. Campaign envelope | Define core themes, playable campaign types, starting era alternatives, PC scopes, iconic adventures | Campaign briefs and representative PCs |
| 3. PC toolbox | Ancestries/backgrounds, professions as examples, Edges/Hindrances, psionic eligibility | Characters can be built without classes; mixed-party issues documented |
| 4. Equipment and powers | Terran small arms, heavy weapons, armor, engineering, psionic and Protoss technologies | Infantry and heavy armor threats function consistently |
| 5. Minimal setting rules | StarCraft-specific exceptions to SWADE only where required | Concise player reference with rationale and examples |
| 6. Pilot profiles | A short bestiary and vehicles sufficient for a frontier threat | Three or more encounter roles across threat scales |
| 7. Pilot adventure and testing | Run the proposed Silent Colony frontier scenario with multiple characters | Document speed, difficulty, lethality, and lore fidelity |
| 8. Systematic expansion | Faction bestiaries, species/variants, groundcraft, aircraft, spacecraft | Lore-source and mechanical consistency reviews |
| 9. Campaign book | Savage Tales, optional Plot Point Campaigns, detailed settings and NPCs | GM can run sessions from the material |

## First playable slice
**Working premise:** a Terran frontier expedition to a silent mining facility. Supports investigation, social consequences, survival, specialists, powered armor, Zerg enemies, and limited vehicle action. It is a test concept, not an approved adventure script.

## Non-goals
Recreating RTS multiplayer balance; locking equipment to RTS units; making every creature buildable with PC points; deriving one universal damage scale from cinematic visuals; front-loading the entire encyclopedic bestiary.
