# Heavy Metal ↔ Personal Power Armor: Pending Interface

**Status:** Open mechanical scope decision; no new house rule approved.
**Sources:** Sci-Fi Companion pp. 93 and 119–127; Core Heavy Armor/Heavy Weapon/character damage.

## What RAW actually presents
- SFC p.93: **Heavy Metal** assigns classes to Heavy Weapons and vehicles from average Damage+AP and total Toughness. It inflicts direct Wounds on a hit by Class relation and includes a vehicle operator's Out-of-Control procedure.
- SFC p.120: stock **power armor has a listed Class** for Heavy Metal, and grants Heavy Armor.
- SFC pp.120–121: a power-armored **character** suffers Wounds normally, and each Wound produces a suit breach and a suit Critical Hit.
- SFC p.122: Size 2 chassis gives +10 Heavy Armor, Pace 6, suit Strength d12+1; armor increases by +2 per Mod to +16.

**Unresolved:** how universal Heavy Metal direct-Wound/Out-of-Control handling applies to *individual infantry wearers* in personal combat. The existence of listed suit Classes suggests the Companion contemplates their use; do **not** assert the book forbids it. But don't silently apply vehicle results to people.

## Demonstration of the breakpoint
Draft C-14 is **2d10/AP4/Heavy Weapon**, Weapon Class **I** (average 11 + 4 = 15). With illustrative d6 Vigor / base Toughness 5:
| Suit | Total Toughness | Heavy Metal Class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CMC-300 +10 | 15 | I |
| CMC-400 +12 | 17 | II |
| Firebat +14 | 19 | II |
| Marauder +16 | 21 | II |

If Heavy Metal were used against all four **as individual characters**, Class I C-14 vs Class I CMC-300 produces **1d3 Wounds per ordinary hit**; vs Class II CMC-400, **0 Wounds**, or **1 on an attack raise**. Thus +2 Armor would jump from 1–3 automatic Wounds to none, while heavier Firebat/Marauder armor gives no additional protection from this weapon in the Class comparison.

## Three approaches
**A — Test recommendation:** **Normal character damage** for power-armored individuals (including Heavy Weapon eligibility, AP, Shaken, Soak and suit critical hits). **Heavy Metal** against vehicles, walkers, spacecraft. Explicitly decide how suit Classes may function in dedicated mechanized battle scenes later. Maintains smooth +Armor differences; this is a **scope proposal**.

**B — Universal Heavy Metal:** Direct Class Wounds on powered infantry; must decide character Soak, Incapacitation, suit Critical Hits and a sensible replacement for vehicle Out-of-Control. Simpler numerically, exceptionally discontinuous.

**C — Hybrid eligibility:** Heavy Metal Class as an upper/lower eligibility filter but ordinary damage for individuals. Would create another new house rule and risk double counting. Avoid without evidence.

**Next:** test A and B with the candidate C-14, several CMC suits, and actual classed vehicles. Obtain owner's decision on A or alternate before publishing combat rules for all suits.
