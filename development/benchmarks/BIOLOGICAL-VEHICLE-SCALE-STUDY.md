# Monsters as Vehicles — Vehicle Guide p.21 and Savage StarCraft Giant Organism Design

**Reviewed 2026-10-11 | Status:** `reviewed` published guidance, **proposed** project-specific classification and corrected Ultralisk baseline, **not owner-adopted Setting Rules**. Sources inspected locally: *SWADE Vehicle Guide* 2020 **pp.4–7, 12, 21**; *SWADE Core Rules* printed **pp.117–120, 175–179**; *Science Fiction Companion* printed **pp.58–63, 93–95, 174, 187–189, 242, 288**; previous [Ultralisk draft](../../bestiary/zerg/ULTRALISK-PLAYTEST.md). Primary StarCraft source: https://classic.battle.net/scc/zerg/units/ultralisk.shtml , official https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/ut.shtml ; RTS numbers/tags never treated as canonical biological measurements.

## What the Vehicle Guide actually says, and what it does not
The dedicated section **"Monsters as Vehicles" (Vehicle Guide p.21)** **explicitly encourages using vehicle conversion tables to design giant monsters or alien creatures**. The user correctly recalled this. It does **not** say that all giant monsters must use the complete vehicle-combat subsystem. Its worked example:
1. Choose the **type of weapon players should need to injure the creature**; weapon expectation guides protection.
2. To estimate average damage, sum **half each die's maximum value plus 0.5 per damage die**, add AP, and optionally **+3.5** (raise allowance). Printed example heavy laser **4d10/AP30** = **20+2+30+3.5 =55.5**, presented as approximately **55 (30)** target Toughness/Armor comparison (rounding is illustrative).
3. Match the resulting Toughness against a **future-era vehicle table**, choose a plausible **Size and dimension**.
4. Return to Core creature **Size Table** and add its **Strength** and biological special abilities. Example: a mutant alien dinosaur **Size10, around 63ft and Strength d12+10**.

**Interpretation:** This is **vehicle-calibrated creature design**, not proof the creature literally has a pilot, mechanical Handling, a vehicle engine or a crew. The guide's opening says its tables are **rough conversion references**, not a balanced points-construction formula (pp.4–5). They are not an instruction to import every future combat tank's Armor to any equally large animal.

**Contrast to user's Strength concern:** Core Size p.179 and the Guide itself use **Strength d12+N** for very large creatures; the Guide's example expressly **retains d12+10**, it doesn't solve perceived plus-N Strength escalation by eliminating the die. For particularly large creatures, designers may give dangerous attacks a **fixed high-class damage profile** as a *separate explicit homebrew choice* while still using Strength for shoves/carrying, but that is **not the Guide's mandatory rule**.

## Three distinct conversion routes — do not conflate
| Route | Use when | Defense & Wounds | Creature-specific rules retained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A: Conventional creature** | Zergling–Roach–Hydralisk–Lurker, and ordinary Ultralisk if adequate | Core **Vigor, Size, natural Armor, Resilient/Huge Wounds, Soak**. Normal rolled damage unless **exceptional Heavy Armor** justified | Fighting, Athletics, movement, wounds/healing/poison, fear, Grappling. **No crew, engine, Out of Control or car-style Critical Hits** |
| **B: Vehicle-calibrated biological creature** (**preferred Ultralisk**)| Living siege predator needs Class-consistent survivability | Choose toughness by **vehicle/walker + peer weapon comparator**, then express via **Core creature** Vigor/Size/Armor and optionally **Heavy Armor**, with Heavy Metal Class from **total Toughness** | Creature mechanics stay. **Size8, Vigor d12, Armor+10 Heavy → Toughness26/ClassIII** is conservative and coherent |
| **C: Full vehicle-resolution bio-colossus** (**optional legendary bosses**) | Nydus transport/worm, airborne Overlord transporter, very large Brutalisk strain, space Leviathan, scripted boss/mass battle | Use actual **vehicle Size/Toughness/Armor/vehicle Wound track**, Heavy Metal and adapted organic **Critical Damage**, and no additional Core Size bonus Wounds | Retain intelligent/feral creature behavior and biological vulnerabilities where relevant. **Do not grant vehicle crew damage, tire/engine failures, Repair-only healing, Piloting or Out-of-Control** to a wild beast without an explicitly suitable organic analogue. Needs an original **biological damage interface** |

The Guide on p.21 directly supports **A→B**; **C** is an **original setting-design possibility**, not the Guide's mandatory instruction. Establish precise damage chart, character Soak eligibility, initiative/agency and healing for C before adopting it for PCs or a default boss. **No second Wound track or stacking Core extra Size Wounds with a separate vehicle Wound total.**

## Mandatory correction to earlier Ultralisk candidate
The source's **SFC Heavy Metal table p.93** defines **Vehicle Class from total Toughness** (I 1–15, II16–25, III26–35, IV36–45, V46–55). The project has explicitly allowed separately listed fixed Class on **powered combat suits** (SFC p.120), **not universally on creatures**. Our old **Ultralisk Size8 +8 Heavy Armor, Toughness24, fixed ClassIII** was internally inconsistent. Do not preserve the old classification without an **explicit extension decision**.

**Preferred provisional **Creature B** repair:** Keep **Size8 Huge, Str d12+8, Vigor d12**, raise exceptional *natural* Heavy Armor from **+8 to +10**; Toughness becomes **8 base Vigor +8 Size +10 Armor = 26 (10)**; now **ClassIII follows RAW p.93** and C-14 ClassI is entirely ineffective without a vulnerability. No change to its Kaiser blade **Str+d10/AP12 (ClassIII)**. Armor+10 also now triggers the SFC **AP-warhead armor≥10 Class-reduction rule** used by our special K12 Punisher: vs Ultralisk III→II, **ClassI K12** gets 0 direct Wounds ordinarily and **1 on raise**, not guaranteed 1d3. This gives armor-piercing ammunition *some* specialist play without outclassing ClassIII rockets. This is a testable **design candidate**, not canon chitin thickness.

**Alternate inferred Size model:** Keeping **Armor+8** but taking **Size10** with **Vigor d12** yields **Toughness26 (8)** and natural ClassIII, plus Strength should move into Core's **d12+7 through +10** range (e.g. d12+10). **However Size10 implies ~63ft, mass up to125tons** per Core p.179. There is not sufficient primary source evidence that an average Ultralisk has that exact mass/length, so don't change Size merely to game the Class threshold.

**Optional full Vehicle C siege-monster:** Build a special *legendary* Ultralisk as **SFC Medium Brawler analogue, Size8, Toughness30(10), ClassIII and five total vehicle Wounds**, and translate its systems into **organic locomotion, ruptured gland/nerve, torn blade, internal hemorrhage**. Do **not** also give Huge creature +2 Wounds; do not use 5 vehicle Wounds plus biological Soak automatically. Distinguish from regular Ultralisk; this is an **alternative future boss**, not adopted regular species stats.

## Fixed weapon class vs enormous Strength — explicit alternative
When Strength climbs to d12+12 or d12+20, **Str+weapon die** creates very high rolled damage, and SFC Heavy Metal may shift weapon Class as a byproduct. This is *not* an error in the Core creature rule; it may be inappropriate for a fixed-function **biological weapon** in this particular sci-fi arms ecosystem. A future **bio-siege attack** might instead list **fixed heavy-weapon damage** like a vehicle cannon (e.g., `3d12/AP12, HW/ClassIII` avg **19.5+12=31.5**, ClassIII) and use Strength separately for raw physical tasks. **This requires a homebrew explicit attack**, not an automatic replacement of every claw, stomp or wrestling action. Keep Ultralisk's current Str+d10 Kaiser blades until tests compare both.
 
## Which Zerg forms get what?
- **Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Baneling:** A **normal creatures**; no advantage from vehicle Wounds/handling.
- **Lurker:** A creature with Burrow and **custom linear attack**, Size4 Large; whole vehicle damage chart is an especially poor fit unless a giant evolved siege Lurker is introduced.
- **Ordinary Ultralisk:** Prefer **B vehicle-calibrated creature, Toughness26(10), ClassIII**, retains Fighting/Sweep/Giant Foes/Soak/organic healing. Evaluate **C** only for boss strains.
- **Brutalisk or evolved Omega/colossus-class Zerg:** **B→C candidate** as future individual lore and size justify.
- **Nydus Worm / enormous transport bioform:** **C potentially best**, with interior transport/corridors/tunnelling and vulnerable tissue, but must define how living passengers interact with its Wounds.
- **Overlord transport variant:** Vehicle passenger/cargo and movement rules may be a natural **C** implementation; don't force all Overlords to be 500-ton battleships.
- **Zerg Leviathan / mobile space organism:** **C high-confidence mechanical genre choice**, as starship-scale biology, with organism-specific systems. Neither SWADE starship numbers nor StarCraft lengths have been sourced in this study.

## Priority tests
1. **Marine rifle vs ordinary Ultralisk:** ClassI C14 vs corrected ClassIII: **No Effect**, no raise exception.
2. **Marauder special K12 Punisher AP vs +10 armor Ultralisk:** target ClassIII→II; ClassI vs II **0 normal, 1 on raise**; grenades also consume ammo.
3. **Class II anti-materiel rifle vs Class III bioarmor:** one level below **0 normal, 1 on raise**.
4. **Class III portable rocket vs Ultralisk III:** **1d3 Wounds** on a hit, +1 attack raise, subject to normal creature Soak and Size Wounds. With 5 vehicle Wounds optional boss, **use different Wound rules and state them**.
5. **Melee Ultralisk Kaiser III vs SFC main tank IV:** **0 Wounds on hit, 1 on raise** (exception possible targeting vulnerable tracks) whereas medium Brawler III targets get 1d3. To defeat hardened main battle tanks regularly, a larger/stronger siege strain needs explicit upgraded blades, not a clandestine Class upgrade.
6. **Creature Huge+Size Wounds versus actual vehicle five Wounds:** compare expected attacks/time-to-disable, Critical Hit workload, Repair versus Healing/Regeneration and rider/cover interaction. It may be unnecessary to switch default Ultralisk to full C.

**Developer note:** The core powered-suit **fixed Class**, ordinary **wearer Soak**, and **one suit Critical Hit per independent post-Soak wound attack** decisions remain untouched. No equivalent universal bio-vehicle rule has been owner-approved.

## Cross-faction evidence: mechanical walkers vs biological transports (2026-10-11)
The [Dragoon/Goliath/Ultralisk comparison](DRAGOON-GOLIATH-ULTRALISK-COMPARISON.md) makes **two equal Size4/ClassII/four vehicle-Wound actual piloted walkers** distinct from the **Size8/ClassIII organic Ultralisk** (natural Fighting and creature Wounds). A further [Overlord/Nydus transport comparison](../../vehicles/zerg/TRANSPORT-BIOFORMS-COMPARISON.md) shows that the **strongest argument for importing vehicle subsystems to living organisms is passenger capacity, boarding, collision/evacuation and network logistics**, not Strength d12+8 alone. Overlords with **Ventral Sacs** can use select passenger/air-vehicle procedures while retaining creature body Wounds if desired; a full bio-vehicle variant would need **one chosen Wound track and biological Critical Hits**. Original SC1 Nydus Canal is a fixed biological route/structure, **not** a roaming transport hull; SC2 emerging Nydus Worm can be a distinct destructible insertion-point creature/structure.
