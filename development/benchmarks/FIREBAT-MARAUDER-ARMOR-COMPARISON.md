# Firebat CMC-660 vs Marauder 5-4 — Armor Evidence Comparison

**Research date:** 2026-10-10.  
**Status:** Reviewed first-pass *comparison*, not a new numeric approval. Existing playtest equipment profiles are preserved.  
**Scope:** Distinguish **hull/ballistic armor**, **flame insulation**, **shock transmission to wearer**, **total suit weight**, **RTS defensive abstractions**, and **licensed miniatures rules**. Do not treat any single game's hit points or Armor as a physical measurement.

## 1. Blizzard lore / primary fiction

| Evidence | Finding | Provenance | Strength and limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *StarCraft* original manual, p. 35, Firebat | **CMC-660 Heavy Combat Suit** is **significantly heavier than Marine armor** and provides **additional heat/flame protection** | `SC98-MANUAL`, Blizzard P1 | **Direct/high** for comparison **against Marine**, does **not compare to post-Brood War Marauder** |
| Blizzard original Firebat unit guide | Firebat power suits are **heavier, more durable, and much more heat resistant than Marine armor** | `SC98-BNET-FIREBAT`, https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/ufire.shtml | **Direct/high**; confirms more ordinary durability than Marine armor |
| *StarCraft: Remastered* art commentary | Firebat visual identity includes thick plating and exposed fuel/plasma apparatus | Blizzard https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/20695698/remastering-starcraft-s-art | **Direct/high** for visual design, does not give ballistic rating |
| *StarCraft II* Marauder technical writeup, quoted by fandom/forum records | The **5-4** is a **modified Firebat CMC-660** of **roughly equal weight**, exchanging part of its **fire insulation** for **thicker neosteel plating**, and fuel tanks for ammunition-loader power generation | Matching archival quote reproductions: https://forums.spacebattles.com/threads/starcraft-source-thread.230914/page-69 and https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/CMC_Powered_Combat_Suit | **Secondary transcription of apparently official source: compelling but original publication not reverified**; do not present as personally inspected Blizzard original. "Same weight" is total assembly mass, not proof of same protection against all threats |
| *Wings of Liberty* Armory description, **Juggernaut Plating** | Reinforces Firebat weak points and thickens helmet/chest, giving small-arms resilience; **+2 game Armor** | Secondary transcription of **in-game** description https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/939643-starcraft-ii-wings-of-liberty/faqs/60938, `ARM-I3`; game-origin P1 but this transcription **not independently captured** | Strong for **armored-up Firebat variant**; not every standard CMC-660 automatically has Juggernaut |
| *Wings of Liberty* Armory description, **Kinetic Foam** | Marauder armor can survive impacts that injure the wearer; foam undersuit absorbs/disperses impulse; **+25 game HP** | Same transcribed in-game material, `ARM-I4` | Strong for shock transfer and upgrade role; it is **not proof of universally thicker armor** |
| *Stealing Thunder* (Micky Neilson) | Marauder defeats incoming gauss spikes; a Firebat's thermal attack remains a serious danger | Official announcement: https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/9989828/new-starcraft-lore-short-story-stealing-thunder ; narrative passages read through third-party reproduction in `lore/entries/terran/powered-combat-suits.md` | P2 story, secondary reproduction of scene: **medium-high**, not numeric threshold |
| *Sector Six* (Micky Neilson) | A penetrating gauss spike into a Firebat fuel/plasma feed can produce catastrophic fire after activation | Blizzard-hosted original https://media.blizzard.com/sc2/lore/sector-six/sector-six.pdf , web indexed excerpt; **full publication not directly reexamined in this pass** | Specific weak-point/fuel-system hazard, **not** blanket flame vulnerability |

**Primary-lore conclusion:** The CMC-660 is demonstrably **more durable than a Marine suit**, *not just flame-resistant*. The **Marauder is a similarly heavy derived combat suit** reportedly favoring thicker ballistic plating, while the Firebat devotes substantial protective mass to thermal insulation. An upgraded **Juggernaut Firebat** can itself receive additional strong small-arms protection. No canonical printed +Armor, exact Class, physical mass of 5-4, or absolute Firebat-versus-Marauder winner is established.

| Unit + game mode | HP | Base armor | Relevant mechanic |

Sources: https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/Firebat ; https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/Marauder_%28Wings_of_Liberty_Campaign%29 ; https://liquipedia.net/starcraft2/Marauder_%28Legacy_of_the_Void%29 ; https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/939643-starcraft-ii-wings-of-liberty/faqs/60938 .

**RTS conclusion:** Baseline WoL campaign Firebat and Marauder are numerically equally durable by basic HP/armor (100/1), then **the Firebat upgrade increases armor rating**, while **Marauder upgrade increases HP/occupant shock survivability**. Do **not** claim standard 125-HP multiplayer Marauder versus 100-HP campaign Firebat is a balanced same-mode armor comparison. The Firebat and Marauder do not coexist as standard choices in the same SC2 multiplayer rules.

## 3. Archon *StarCraft: Tabletop Miniatures Game* — licensed derivative

**Public card evidence:** Official May 2026 Terran P2P cards https://starcraft-tmg.com/files/downloads/StarCraft-Terran-P2P-Card-Sheets-A4_EN.pdf, **PDF p. 1 Marine / p. 2 Marauder**. September 2026 W2 cards also checked: https://starcraft-tmg.com/files/downloads/StarCraft-Terran-P2P-W2-Card-Sheets-A4_EN.pdf. Rules meanings: https://starcraft-tmg.com/files/downloads/StarCraft-TMG-Quick-Start-Rules_EN.pdf, **p. 3–4**. Values are **wargame mechanics**, not actual thickness/rating.

| Unit | HP/model | Armour save | Combat tag | Upgrade |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| **Marine** | **2** | **5+** | **Light** | Combat Shield: different Evade defense, not +Armor |
| **Marauder** | **5** | **4+** | **Armoured** | **Kinetic Foam: +1 HP**; Veteran of Tarsonis **+1 Armour** while near objective |
| **Firebat** | **unavailable in released public sheets** | **unavailable** | **unavailable** | No published Firebat card verified |

Lower Armour target number is **better** (4+ succeeds on 4–6 versus 5+ on 5–6), as the Quick Start defines. The wargame therefore supports Marauder > Marine durability, **but does not provide Firebat ↔ Marauder numerical comparison yet**. A March 2026 third-party release-roadmap report speculated Firebat in **December wave**, subject to change: https://www.wargamer.com/starcraft-tabletop-miniatures-game/release-schedule ; as of 2026-10-10 **no Firebat listing/card is verified in the official downloads**. Main TMG Core Rules use a Firebat with a Flamer Template as an illustrative example, but that does **not** establish its unit-card Armor.

The **Marauder Kinetic Foam +HP** mirrors its SC2 WoL campaign upgrade in qualitative role. Do not equate TMG HP with SWADE Toughness or infer exact weight from miniatures/base size.

## 4. Implications for Savage StarCraft — keep approved provisional baseline

| Suit profile | Provisional baseline already owner-accepted | Interpretive role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CMC-300/400 | +10 Heavy Armor, **fixed Class I** | Standard Marine armor |
| CMC-660 | +14 Heavy Armor, **fixed Class II** | **Heavy protective shell + strong thermal specialization** |
| 5-4 | +16 Heavy Armor, **fixed Class II** | **More plating / kinetic-focused**, less specialized thermal insulation |

**Leave these values intact as `playtest` baselines.** The `+14` versus `+16` difference is a **plausible SWADE conversion guess**, NOT lore-proven precise material advantage and doesn't change their current shared Class II under Heavy Metal. **A Juggernaut-plated Firebat variant could test +16 Heavy Armor with Class II unchanged** (one extra Armor Mod from +14 on an SFC Size 2 frame; total three Armor Mods); don't silently assign it to every Firebat.

The Firebat still needs a **separate heat/flame-specific protection** under published SWADE mechanics rather than rebranding Armor/Heavy Metal Class as thermal immunity. The Marauder **Kinetic Foam** needs careful mechanical selection: a generic SFC `Toughness +1` Mod **does not alter Heavy Metal's Class-relative direct Wounds**, so by itself it fails to reproduce Kinetic Foam's function *against classed weapons*. Consider existing character injury/Soak/critical-effect precedents later, and avoid inventing a special new subsystem without need.

**Next actual equipment design:** paired Perdition flame system, Firebat thermal defensive module, Marauder K12 ordnance, optional Juggernaut plate and kinetic foam. Do not individually freeze final equipment stats before the integrated playtest roster exists.
