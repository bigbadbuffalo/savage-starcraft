# Protoss Ground Combat — Armor, Shields, Psionics and Range Source Survey

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-11. **Status:** `reviewed` for cited published rules and primary source content; all proposed stats remain `draft/playtest`, not owner-approved final designs.  
**Intent:** Differentiate **physical suit plating**, **technological shield generators**, **innate psionic aptitude**, **trained active powers**, **psi-channeled weapons**, and **robotic platforms**. Don't automatically import Terran CMC Heavy Armor Mods/Class, RTC HP/shield numbers, TMG wound pools, or "all Protoss are High Templar" power budgets.

RTS values are tactical-role indicators, **not physical damage or armor measurements**.

## Archon tabletop game — P4 mechanical comparator
**Official printable May 2026** [Protoss cards](https://starcraft-tmg.com/files/downloads/StarCraft-Protoss-P2P-Card-Sheets-A4_EN.pdf), screenshot checked in current review, explicitly separates **HP, Armour, Shield**, movement/speed and special abilities. Example: **basic Zealot / Praetor Guard** PDF p.3 (zero-index p.2) has **Shield 3, Armour 5+**, psi blades with anti-large mechanics; **Stalker** PDF p.6 (zero-index p.5) has **Shield 3, Armour 4+**, anti-armour Particle Disruptors. **Sentry** cards include Guardian Shield, a placed Force Field and Hallucination. **Adept** uses Glaive Cannon and Psionic Transfer. These distinctions are good **role corroboration**, **not** SWADE shield charges or armor values. The cards distinguish biological Protoss from **Mechanical** units; they do not prove an energy shield is natural bodily toughness. SFC conversions should not map a TMG shield "3" to three SWADE Wounds.

## Critical cross-faction rules question: Heavy Metal

**The project's Heavy Metal interpretation assigns fixed Class to actual Classed power armor.** A Zealot in **SFC +6 *ordinary* battle armor + Personal Force Field (+4 Toughness)** does **not** automatically have Heavy Armor or a fixed Class. Against a Marine C-14, **use conventional 2d10/AP4 rolled damage against Toughness** (subtract AP from actual ordinary Armor), not direct Heavy Metal class Wounds. A shield that adds +4 Toughness helps as intended, and the worn plating is not equivalent to a Terran CMC.

**But:** StarCraft shield fiction describes **finite depleting and recharging energy reserves** even against classed attacks. To get that and protected Protoss **Heavy Armor/Vehicle** interactions right in advanced variants, test an alternative **SFC p.123 charge-based Electronics Soak generator** as a separately approved technology conversion. Do **not stack +4 Toughness and automatic free recharge/Soak charges** by silently treating two different published devices as a single standard issue emitter.

**Melee class issue:** Native **Psi Blade is Class I**. Blizzard explicitly describes Zealots cutting armored vehicles. A **higher-output Templar psi blade** may reasonably be **Class II**, but that requires a separate user-reviewed variant of the published Psi Blade (e.g. improved AP and die type plus the specific wielder/suit Strength used for Class calculation), **not blanket +4 AP to every Protoss hand weapon**. The proposed Zealot stat entry makes this clear.

## Strategic research conclusions / testing
1. **Zealots wear literal lore power suits** but the **SFC worn-armor + Personal Force Field** model may fit their battlefield role with less Heavy Metal complexity than a CMC frame. Armor and shield remain separate purchased/lost/damaged gear.
2. **Innate Protoss psionic affinity** is not **universal Arcane Background (Psionics)**. Give broad baseline communicative telepathy and specialist training routes; distinguish Khala historical membership from natural capacity and Nerazim void disciplines.
3. A **shielded melee attacker must survive approaching rifle squads**, yet not get blanket invulnerability from two simultaneously stacked shield systems. Evaluate charging under cover, Pace/Speed, Bennies and normal Shooting tactics.
4. **Zealot** and **Dark Templar** are active blade specialists; **Adept** is mobile light ranged psionic; **Stalker** is biomechanical/robotic ranged skirmisher; **Sentry** is robotic support; **High Templar** spells use PP and casting; **Dragoon** is walker-scale (defer full vehicle stats).
5. **No source supports every individual Protoss receiving 3 spells and 10 Power Points**. That would erase important lore distinctions in castes, soldiers, and trained Templar.
6. **No published personal force field in SFC currently depletes after *each successful hit* or slowly restores *that buffer* just like StarCraft.** We either accept approximation (candidate A) or explicitly design a small shield-resource adaptation (candidate B) after comparative tests.

## Subsequent native walker shields discovery (2026-10-11)
**SFC p.185 has a dedicated Walker Shields Modification**, in addition to p.123 power-armor Shields and p.42 personal Force Field. For an actual **Size4, four-Wound Dragoon walker**, published Walker Shields cost **2 Mods** and provide **2 automatically spent Electronics-based Soak charges**, recharged outside battle over 10 minutes. Therefore **walker Dragoon plasma shields require no detached-device homebrew**, though exact Protoss recharge flavor and the pilot's Electronics/psionic interface remain design choices. This *does not* settle the Zealot's physical suit/shield or the Stalker's disputed **Size2 (power armor range) versus Size4+ walker** mechanical frame. Details: [Dragoon profile](../../vehicles/protoss/DRAGOON-WALKER-PLAYTEST.md); [three-way study](DRAGOON-GOLIATH-ULTRALISK-COMPARISON.md).
