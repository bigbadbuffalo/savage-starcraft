# CMC-660 Firebat and Marauder 5-4 — Size 2 versus Size 3 Chassis Review

**Date:** 2026-10-10  
**Status:** **Size 3 is owner-approved for both CMC-660 Firebat and 5-4 Marauder** (2026-10-10). The existing provisional +14/Class II Firebat and +16/Class II Marauder protection numbers remain unchanged. Weapon/hardpoint profiles still need to be specified.
**Question:** Arm-integrated twin Perdition flamethrowers and arm-mounted Quad K12 Punisher launchers need capacity for weapons, fuel/ammunition and specialized protective systems. Is SFC Size 3 a better *frame* than Size 2?

## What the published Science Fiction Companion actually says

**SFC, printed p. 120:** Power armor has explicit **Mods** capacity, including normal Power Armor Modifications **and vehicular weapons** (reference to p. 58). The published **Size 2 Assault Suit** on pp. 120, 126 has **+16 Heavy Armor/Class II**, six full Mods, including 3× Armor, Integrated Gear (shoulder grenade launcher), Jump Pack and Trauma Pack; a mounted launcher does not by itself force Size 3.

**SFC, printed p. 125:** **Integrated Gear** costs **1 Mod** per permitted built-in package and handles up to **10 lb of gear**. It is not a general license to install arbitrary 15+ lb guns or a self-fabricating ammunition factory without Mod expenditure. A **personal Heavy Flamer** is **15 lb** on p. 52—too heavy for *one* default 10 lb Integrated Gear allowance.

**SFC, printed p. 60 (Vehicular Weapons):** **Vehicular Heavy Flamethrower** is **1 Mod, Min Size 1**, while **Vehicular Grenade Launcher** is **1 Mod, Min Size 1** (with an included ammo load/reload as stated). These can be mounted on a Size 2 or Size 3 compatible suit under power armor's vehicle-weapons allowance, subject to the exact weapon/loadout rules. Two separately mounted weapons would tentatively use **two weapon Mods**. Linked Weapons on printed p. 45 have extra Mod expense for additional linked weapons, so whether each StarCraft multi-barrel assembly is one SWADE weapon, linked barrels, or two separate shots must be addressed when specific profiles are written. **No automatic extra weapon Mods are granted just by saying “built in.”**

**SFC, printed p. 127:** **Fire Support Suit** is an explicit **Size 3, +18 Armor/Class II, nine-Mod** powered-armor model equipped with a medium slugthrower and a dual-linked light autocannon. This is closer in armament density to full assault suits with integral heavy weapons, but is not proof that a StarCraft suit must be Size 3.

**SFC p. 121:** **Size 4+** power armor becomes a **walker**; Size 3 remains personal power armor with the established worn-suit damage rules. Suit construction **Size does not automatically increase the wearer's natural Size or Toughness** in this conversion. Each suit retains its printed **fixed Class**, chosen as equipment data, not dynamic operator Toughness.

| Frame parameter | Size 2 | Size 3 | Delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Base Heavy Armor | +10 | +12 | +2 |
| Armor cap | +16 | +20 | +4 |
| Mod capacity | 6 | 9 | **+3** |
| Pace | **6** | **4** | **−2** |
| Suit Strength | d12+1 | d12+2 | +1 step equivalent |
| Unpowered weight | 200 lb | 300 lb | +100 lb |
| Generic SFC frame cost | $100K | $150K | +$50K |

**At *equal desired protection***, the larger frame additionally saves one Armor Mod:
- **Firebat +14:** Size 2 uses **2 Armor Mods**; Size 3 uses **1**.
- **Marauder +16:** Size 2 uses **3 Armor Mods**; Size 3 uses **2**.

Thus switching from Size 2 to Size 3 yields **four more slots available to other systems at unchanged +14/+16 Heavy Armor** (+3 intrinsic capacity and −1 Armor Mod). Neither suit needs to jump to +18/+20 simply because its frame is bigger.

## Lore compatibility and real costs
- **Blizzard original unit text:** Firebat carries **twin arm-mounted Perdition flamethrowers**, https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/ufire.shtml (official P1). Marauder carries arm-mounted Quad K12 guns and an auto-loader (SC2 Armory transcript https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/939643-starcraft-ii-wings-of-liberty/faqs/60938 ; later-source references at https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/CMC_Powered_Combat_Suit). **Both armaments are physically attached to the suits**; they are not interchangeable handheld small arms by default.
- A later technical account (original still unverified) characterizes Marauder and Firebat suits as of **roughly comparable overall weight** despite neosteel-vs-insulation differences. If changing frame Size, **move both models together** to remain consistent; do not make Marauder Size 3 and Firebat Size 2 on weapon-count grounds alone.
- **Size 3's default Pace is 4, versus Size 2 Pace 6.** This is a meaningful tabletop penalty and may fit slower shock infantry, but not necessarily every game/fictional depiction. If required, the **published Increased Pace Mod, p.124, adds +2 Pace and increases the running die** at 1 Mod. Using it to restore Pace 6 has a real slot cost and also alters running performance. Avoid granting compensating speed for free.
- A larger frame has **higher generic cost and unpowered mass**; these are Companion construction analogues, not canonical StarCraft numbers.
- **No added Armor Class, Wound capacity, automatic scaling defense or wearer Size/Toughness** just because the chassis is Size 3. The previously approved **fixed Class II** remains an equipment selection.

## Recommendation and status
**Prefer Size 3 for both suits as the next equipment-design *working candidate*** if they use substantive installed twin heavy weapon systems and still need meaningful Mods for heat defense/foam, targeting or autoloading. SFC's Size 3 Fire Support Suit provides strong printed precedent.

**Not forced by RAW:** The published Size 2 Assault Suit already carries a grenade launcher, and p.60 Min Size 1 weapons fit a Size 2 chassis. **Size 2 remains viable** if the models intentionally sacrifice customization for compactness, limited weapons or no expensive manufacturing/extra defense. We should not enlarge suits solely to skirt a hard 10-lb limit on `Integrated Gear`—using the published vehicular weapon mounting rules is an independent and more direct solution.

**Owner decision (2026-10-10):** **Adopt Size 3 on the current provisional CMC-660 and 5-4 suit equipment entries**. The updated CMC gear table now uses one Armor Mod for +14 Firebat, two Armor Mods for +16 Marauder, and nine total slots each. The illustrative twin weapon mounts remain estimates until actual Perdition/K12 weapon construction. Firebat fire resistance remains a **draft** proposal using Core Environmental Resistance (Heat); see `../../equipment/terran/FIRE-RESISTANT-INSULATION-DRAFT.md`. Preserve prior Size 2 budgets as historical comparisons only.
