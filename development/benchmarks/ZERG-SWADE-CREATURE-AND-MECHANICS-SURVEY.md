# Zerg ground organisms — shape-first, cross-Companion mechanics survey

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Status:** `reviewed` published SWADE comparators and primary Blizzard roles; numerical conversion proposals are `playtest` only.  
**Scope:** Core Rules, **all four** supplied Companions (Fantasy, Horror, Science Fiction, Super Powers), Vehicle Guide, World Builder/GM Guide, and the licensed Alternity StarCraft scan. Compare anatomy/scale **before** selecting powers. Do not equate StarCraft unit selection size, model size, or RTS HP with canonical length/mass/SWADE Size.  
**Size authority:** *SWADE Core Rules*, **printed p.179 Size Table** (PDF p.181). Size/Toughness bonus is keyed principally to **mass**, not limb-to-limb length. The separately supplied **`Unit_Sizes.pdf`** (spreadsheet-derived measurements) may be useful as **unverified research hypotheses** only; it has no demonstrated source provenance. In particular the 36'/84' Ultralisk variants are **not** treated as Blizzard-certified dimensions.

## 3. Unlikely-source mechanical mining — all Companions

### Core Rules — physical creature primitives (pp.175–189)
- **Burrow** printed **pp.175–176**: tunnel movement, ambush opposed Stealth versus Notice, Vulnerable or The Drop, and possibility of attacks from beneath surface. **Use** for Lurker and optional Zergling/Roach burrowing; stationary attack requires a specific Lurker constraint.
- **Regeneration** printed **p.177**: Fast attempt *each round* (Vigor; heals one Wound success, extra with raise); Slow once a day. **Conditional Roach burrowed regeneration** is a justified adaptation but could become extremely strong, especially with Resilient; measure as a system.
- **Resilient / Very Resilient** printed **p.178**: one/two extra Wounds for **Extras**. **Use** for durable Roach, not default every Zerg or Wild Card; **Large/Huge Size already adds extra Wounds**, so don't add for Ultralisk thoughtlessly.
- **Size table** **p.179**: Size4-7 = Large +1 Wound, Reach+1; Size8-11 = Huge +2 Wounds, Reach+2; Size12+ Gargantuan +3 Wounds, Reach+3. **Use real Size** rather than importing RTS Small/Medium/Large.
- **Swarms** **p.189**: area-template vermin, often immune to piercing attacks. **Do not** model ordinary Zergling mobs as a literal Swarm template: each is a dog-sized attackable individual, and a sealed CMC should *not* make Zerglings unable to attack it by activating Tiny-Vermin Swarm immunity.

### Fantasy Companion — surprises from creatures and powers
- **Velociraptor** **p.188**: tactical pack carnivore with Size1 and Armor+2. **Good alternate physical Zergling base** when a larger Brood/Devouring One variant is intended.
- **Giant Scorpion** **pp.207–208**: Str d12+1, Armor+4, Size3, Frenzy; excellent Hydralisk starting body, with claws/spine substitution.
- **Gnoll Pack Tactics** **p.209**: adds Gang Up bonus to **Fighting damage**. Excellent high-impact alternative to generic +1 Fighting for coordinated Zerglings. It is **not a universal bonus to all coordinated Shooting**.
- **War Elephant** **p.196**: Sweep (Imp), Hardy and trample/charge physical mass. **Better fight behavior for Ultralisk than merely increasing Str/Toughness**.
- **Giant Spider Queen/Great Spider** **p.208**: web range/templates with Entangled/Bound, natural chitin; useful **future Infestor parasite/web variants**, not proof vanilla Hydralisks shoot webs.
- **Acid Spray** power **p.71**: option for alien bioacid Trappings; **not a free automatic armor destruction rule**. A magical power uses arcane skill/PPs unless explicitly converted to innate biological equipment.

### Horror Companion — dangerous organisms, contamination and counterplay
- **Corpse Worm Swarm** **pp.112–113**: parasites **burrow into victims after Shaken/Wounded** and cause ongoing injury; potentially good **Infestor/parasite** or infected ecosystem hazard, not a standard Zergling bite.
- **Giant Spider / other ambush horrors** in **Creature** chapter **pp.160–162**: burrow, paralysis or Pounce in a single sheet; **mechanical route for neural attacks** in special organisms.
- **Swarms** **pp.161–162**: swarm attacks damage least armored area, but **fully sealed suits immune** in the printed entry. Strong **anti-parasite sealed-armor counterplay** precedent; *do not* use that to mean CMC armor is immune to all large Zerg bodies.
- **Fear** and psychological hazards in expanded horror rules: Zerg ambush and infestation are naturally appropriate to **ordinary Core Fear** and optional **Horror Companion** expanded fear/madness; do not give every Zerg automatic Fear on sight or permanent sanity rules.

### Science Fiction Companion — the best niche ideas
- **Death Worm** **pp.240–241**: *Burrow 8* and an organic electric **Death Bolt fired from just beneath ground at Near Total Cover**. Excellent **Roach/Lurker delivery and counterplay**, despite the mismatched electric damage.
- **Decapitator** **p.241**: AP4 scythe limbs and natural ambush. Good Hydralisk blades and Hunter Killer strain.
- **Lesser Xenos** **p.236**: 
  - **Firefly:** organic **explosive fire Large Blast** when attracted to intense heat — mechanical *Baneling death-burst* comparator, but the trigger and scale differ.
  - **Crumpler:** metal/plastic-consuming microscopic swarm lowers **object Hardness** over hours — *creep/equipment corrosion* instead of rolling extra rifle damage.
  - **Croaker/Alarm Bird:** subterranean vibrations or circling signal **attract predators** — covert Zerg perimeter alarm / brood-control ecology.
  - **Stygian Midge:** sensor-obscuring Pitch Darkness template, including IR but **not sound/sonar** — a natural *sensor-counterplay* inspiration for spore clouds.
  - **Snowball:** self-replicating vermin become **Difficult Ground** — models massed organisms/rapid infestation better than doubling an individual creature's Wounds.
  - **Spewer/Vomit Bird:** Cone template biological secretions and on-injury acidic retaliation — *specialized* acid strains or hazard ideas.
- **Throxian Soldier** **p.289**: **Hive Mind, Hardy, Resilient, Pounce, Leaper, Spike/Entangle, Wall Walker** on an alien melee infantry unit. Wonderful **behavioral prototype** for Zerg coordinated warriors; **not** physically a Zergling or permission to give every ability to baseline Zerglings.
- **Throxian Enceph** **p.288**: localized **Hive Mind Focus** expanding brood communications via leader. Natural method for **Overlord/Queen/Cerebrate control networks** rather than universal telepathy at infinite range.
- **Throxian Bombard** **p.288**: **Armor+6 Heavy Armor**, powerful organic artillery, Hardy and Resilient on a titanic organism. Supports letting *exceptional* Zerg species earn Heavy Armor; doesn't establish all biological shells are Class III.
- **Bore Worm Swarms** **p.287**: infection through skin, daily untreated harm, medical removal; good *creep infestation hazard* countermeasure: **sealed suits block access** unless breached.

### Super Powers Companion — *mechanic inspiration*, not unlimited enemy power points
- **Burrowing** **p.54**: movement beneath soft surfaces at half Pace, attacks from underground; the **Core creature Burrow** is simpler for Zerg NPCs, so **do not also charge them Super Power Points**.
- **Decay** **p.57**: acid/rust/corrosion with Vigor consequences and stronger version affecting objects/armor. Useful **late-game corrosive variants** but **not** an automatic default on every Roach attack; extra actions, rolls and status consequences matter.
- **Explode** **p.62**: a super detonates with blast but **survives or reconstitutes**; it is **NOT an appropriate direct rules transplant** for a suicidal Baneling. Use its blast design as an inspiration only.
- **Regeneration** **p.81**: costed super-power version with per day/hour/round levels; prefer **Core creature Regeneration** for Roaches, which avoids incompatible SPC power-budget system.
- **Damage Field** **pp.56–57**, Growth **p.64**, Wall Walker **p.91**: likely custom Zerg evolutions, acidic contact hazards, or wall-climbers, but species-specific; don't give huge creature Growth stacks on top of already selected Size.

### Vehicle Guide and World Builder/GM Guide — *useful limits* and encounter design
- **Vehicle Guide:** Chases/collision, Scale, vehicle weapon mounting/critical contexts are **not default creature rules**. A huge Ultralisk is an organic combatant **with creature Size/Wounds**, not a tracked vehicle with Out of Control checks. Use the guide for a **comparative tank/charge benchmark** later, not transposed vehicle handling or operator checks.
- **World Builder/GM Guide:** Environmental hazards, encounter prompts, location complications and Dramatic Tasks are good scaffolding for **creep growth, breached bio-containment and evacuation**, but they do not supply numerical Zerg strain stats. An infestation scene can be an evolving **hazard/Dramatic Task** while individual Zerg use stat blocks.

## 4. Load-bearing conversion principles
1. **Natural Armor and Heavy Armor differ**: baseline Zergling/Hyra/Roach/Lurker thick hide is **ordinary natural Armor**, protecting via Toughness/rolled damage, **not a Heavy Metal Class**. An Ultralisk specifically receives an **unsettled provisional Heavy Armor/Class III creature exception**, motivated by direct fiction and SFC biological Heavy Armor examples; do not expand to all carapaces.
2. **Natural ranged weapons** may be Heavy Weapons **if fiction demands piercing Heavy Armor**, as Hydralisk spines (2 cm neosteel claim) and Lurker reinforced-plate spines do. A *Heavy Weapon* tag is not an automatic consequence of AP, body size, or organic chemistry.
3. **Zerg regeneration is not the same as a free per-round complete heal**. Roach Fast Regeneration while burrowed remains a significant draft hypothesis; ordinary Zerg post-battle repair can use Slow Regeneration or environmental/medical recovery without massive extra accounting.
4. **Coordination doesn't automatically equal permanent telepathic +2 hit**. Normal SWADE Gang Up first; reserve Gnoll Pack Tactics for damage and communication network mechanics for dedicated command organisms.
5. **Baneling one-use acid blast** must remove/incapacitate the detonating organism, unlike SPC's survivable Explode; it cannot also have invulnerable swarm immunity.
6. **Ultralisk classed blade** should genuinely menace Class II/III targets, while ordinary Marine C-14 should be mostly or wholly ineffective against thickest defenses. Distinguish this from ordinary chitin.
7. **Burrow vs cover**: Lurker's spines require being buried but can still be hit by area effects, detected/scanned, targeted via revealed position or attacked after it surfaces; don't grant untouchable invisibility by default.
8. **Three books can represent one ability in differing ways**: use **Core** movement/Size/Resilient/Regeneration first, **Fantasy/Horror/SFC** creature special abilities second, **Super Powers** only if something genuinely extraordinary isn't represented in an NPC-compatible mechanism.
9. **Lore evidence does not quantify exact Size, Armor, Wounds, damage dice or range**. These are provisional creature building numbers and should be judged only in integrated Marine/Firebat/Marauder/Ghost/vehicle/Protoss scenarios.
