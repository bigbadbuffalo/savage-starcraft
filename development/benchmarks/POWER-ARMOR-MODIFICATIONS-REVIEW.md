# Firebat CMC-660 and Marauder 5-4 — Published Power Armor Modifications Review

**Research date:** 2026-10-10. **Status:** Reviewed official SWADE rulebook options; **mechanical recommendations, not new approved item Mods**. The already owner-accepted provisional CMC Armor/Class values are unchanged.

**Primary rules source:** *SWADE Science Fiction Companion*, printed pp. **119–127**, especially **120 (power-armor profiles), 122 (frames / negative and core Mods), 123 (defensive Mods), 124 (Trauma Pack/Toughness), 125 (offensive Mods), 126–127 (stock suit examples)**; also p. **42 (personal force field and environmental suits), 45 (Incendiary), 52 (flamers), 93 (Heavy Metal)**; SWADE Core **p. 127 (Fire hazard)**. Original StarCraft lore and SC2 upgrades: `FIREBAT-MARAUDER-ARMOR-COMPARISON.md`.

## Published properties versus actual Modifications
- **Basic powered suit (SFC p.119):** Already **sealed**, maintains life support and heating/cooling, and protects against environmental temperature, toxins, atmosphere etc. This is a **shared baseline** and not a separate purchase. It does **not explicitly grant immunity to flame-weapon attacks or burning fuel**.
- **Self-Sealing (p.123):** **1 Mod**. Automatically seals suit breach after a Wound except when **3+ Wounds from one attack**. Important for Firebat's hazardous role and any vacuum/chemical battlefield. It is **not a substitute for flame resistance** and does not eliminate vehicle-like fuel/plasma-line vulnerabilities.
- **Armor (p.123):** **1 Mod for +2 Heavy Armor**; SFC Size 2 frame +10, max +16. Our CMC-660 +14 already uses **two Armor Mods**; Marauder +16 uses **three**. Both have their owner-accepted candidate fixed **Class II**, so +2 Armor **does not further reduce the Class I C-14's direct Heavy Metal Wounds**. It still matters for any normal damage roll, if the attack is eligible to affect Heavy Armor, and signals physical plating.
- **Toughness (p.124):** **1 Mod per +1 Toughness**, max 5. Mechanically represents reinforced frame/general protection; **does not alter a fixed suit Heavy Metal Class dynamically or protect from Class-relative direct Wounds**. Avoid calling this an exact substitute for Kinetic Foam.
- **Ballistic Protection (p.123):** **1 Mod**, reduces **rolled damage from slugthrower bullets by 4**. Does not by RAW subtract anything from Heavy Metal direct Wounds. Whether electromagnetically launched C-14 spikes qualify as the book's **slugthrower bullets** is itself a category/adaptation question; even if they do, Heavy Metal remains direct Class/Wound resolution. **Not recommended as primary Marauder vs C-14 defense** without a new rule.
- **Trauma Pack (p.124):** **1 Mod**, ignores up to **2 points of Wound penalties and Fatigue**, adds **+2 on rolls against Bleeding Out**, lasts **one hour after automatic injection**, then drugs must be replaced. This is the **closest directly usable mechanical proxy** for Kinetic Foam's *occupant survival* role, because it helps a badly struck wearer stay operational; **it does not prevent Wounds, create a Soak attempt, absorb a penetrating hit or represent literal impact-absorbing foam**. Could be re-flavored only if distinct mechanism is acknowledged as an adaptation; a Marauder could also carry both trauma drugs and a foam layer.
- **Energy Skin (p.123):** **no Mod slot**, reduces damage from **lasers by 4**, with a sight-based Stealth penalty. **Does not apply to flame, plasma, thermal hazards or incendiary attacks by RAW.** Not a Firebat fire-resistance solution.
- **Shields (p.123):** **half Size Mods**, so 1 Mod for Size 2. A rechargeable field provides a small number of **Electronics-based Soak charges**, distinct from ordinary passive Armor; advanced technology (Z tech indicator). Can genuinely mitigate direct Wounds and might fit Protoss/very advanced equipment, but is **not a lore-faithful default model of passive Marauder foam or Firebat heat insulation** without added setting explanation.
- **EMP Shielding (p.123):** **1 Mod**, limits EMP operation/reset penalties. Useful generic suit survivability but does not resist fire or kinetic damage.
- **Repair Nanomachines (p.123):** **3 Mods**, attempts automated Repair of suit **Critical Hits** only a limited number of times. Does not prevent or heal user Wounds; unlikely default CMC feature.
- **Stabilizer (p.125):** **1 Mod**, cancels recoil/running/Unstable Platform penalties for **attached direct-fire weapons**. A Firebat with **RoF 1 Cone flamers** likely gains little benefit; don't allocate it solely out of habit. Does not cover all carried weapons.
- **Targeting System (p.125):** **1 Mod** removes two points of certain direct-fire Shooting penalties and applies to both **attached and carried** direct-fire weapons; tactically meaningful for suitable Marauder weapons, with target/launcher compatibility to be checked. Does not help guided weapons.
- **Negative Mods (p.122)** such as **Reduced Pace** refund slots at an explicit movement cost but **should not be assumed merely because the armor looks bulky**. **Fragile** imposes extra Critical Hits when Shaken and conflicts with the intended durable suit profiles.

## Crucial published stock precedent
The stock **Assault Suit (Class II)** in the SFC **pp.120, 126** is a **Size 2, +16 Heavy Armor shock infantry suit with a grenade launcher and a Trauma Pack**. This is a much closer structural SWADE model of a **Marauder** than the generic +10 frame. It does **not** imply StarCraft has the exact same grenade launcher, suit mass, jump pack, cost, or pharmaceutical equipment, just a very good mechanical template.

## Proposed Mod allocation — no new approved suit stats
**CMC-660 standard Firebat, +14 Heavy / Class II, Size 2 (six slots):**
- **2 Armor Mods**, **1 Self-Sealing**, **1 Trauma Pack** = **4 / 6 slots**.
- **Two slots reserved** for the flamethrower mount/special protection or mission-specific hardware. The SFC gives **no direct fire-resistant Armor Mod**. If a fire-defense difference is essential against actual flame weapon damage, the minimum scoped setting-specific Mod needs to be researched/designed and then integrated into the whole-equipment playtest packet.
- Consider omitting the existing **Stabilizer** candidate until an attached *direct-fire* weapon that actually uses it is confirmed. Default environmental sealing **already** applies to both CMC-660 and Marauder; the Firebat's extra *relative* flame insulation still lacks a specific numerical RAW Mod.

**Marauder 5-4, +16 Heavy / Class II, Size 2 (six slots):**
- **3 Armor Mods + 1 Trauma Pack** = **4 / 6 slots** as the **minimal direct-RAW, after-trauma model**, with room for specialized autoloader/launcher assistance or **Toughness +1** = fifth slot, which affects conventional damage but not Class-based attacks. A **Targeting System**, if compatible with its direct-fire K12, would use another slot.
- A literal **Kinetic Foam** feature that *prevents* impact Wounds is **not fully represented by any published passive Mod**. Trauma Pack models *survival after impact*, Toughness modestly reinforces non-classed defenses, Shields adds active energy-based Soak. No exact equivalence should be claimed.

**Optional Juggernaut Firebat, +16 Heavy / Class II:** One more Armor Mod on the +14 suit (three total Armor Mods); leaves **3 slots before other features**. This represents the in-game ballistic reinforcement as a distinct variant, not standard-issue to all CMC-660 suits.

## Further interaction to test after equipment batch
The stock **Heavy Flamer** in SFC **p.52** does **4d6 (Class I), Heavy Weapon, Incendiary**, RoF 1, Cone; **Hand Flamer** 3d6 (Class I). Thus direct Heavy Metal Class I flame hits versus Class II Marauder initially produce 0 Wounds, or 1 with raise. The **Incendiary** property (SFC p.45) refers individual-target ignition to Core **p.127**, where armor protects normally unless otherwise stated. An armored Marauder's exposed ammunition/fuel mechanisms can be dangerous through specific weak points/ignition, but do **not** make every standard flamer Class II just because fiction depicts one seriously threatening a Marauder. Investigate fuel-system attacks, area hazards and payload rather than inventing a special damage bypass prematurely.

## Provisional conclusions
1. **Keep +14 Firebat / +16 Marauder as provisional accepted baselines, both fixed Class II.**
2. **Armor** mechanically captures Marauder thicker neosteel; **Trauma Pack** is the best direct native *post-impact* substitute for the *role* of Kinetic Foam, but **cannot model prevention of impact injury**.
3. **Basic Sealed + Self-Sealing** capture environmental resilience, not specialized resistance to enemy flame attacks. **No published Mod directly provides resistance to fire/flamethrower damage**.
4. Build complete Firebat and Marauder offense/utility profiles before committing to any new specialty rule. Only add a small fire-specific protective Mod (or kinetic absorption mechanism) if table-facing interactions demonstrate a real gap.

## Current owner preference (2026-10-10): revise earlier Trauma Pack recommendation
**Correction to the proposed packages above:** The owner questions adding **Trauma Pack** without specific Marauder or Firebat lore. **It should not appear as *recommended standard issue* merely because the stock SFC Assault Suit happens to include one.** It explicitly administers antibiotics/stimulants/anesthetics and **does not** physically cushion impacts. The *StarCraft II* Marauder Kinetic Foam upgrades transmit less shock to the wearer—different technology. **Published Toughness +1** is physically plausible but bypassed in Heavy Metal direct Wounds; **Ballistic Protection** is also plausible against rolled bullet damage but doesn't change class-relative Wounds. Current **custom Kinetic Foam hypothesis:** 1 Mod, +2 to the wearer's **ordinary Soak** against kinetic/concussive impacts, still requiring a Benny; +1 version alternative. The published *Iron Jaw* Edge (Core p.42) is the precedent for a Soak bonus. These are **research alternatives, not owner-adopted new mechanics**. See `MARAUDER-KINETIC-FOAM-STUDY.md`.
