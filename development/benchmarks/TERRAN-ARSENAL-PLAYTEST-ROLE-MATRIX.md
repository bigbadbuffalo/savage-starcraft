# Terran Ground Arsenal — First Playtest Role Matrix (2026-10-10)

**Status:** Equipment/ecology smoke-check; **not** an actual tabletop encounter and **not final balance approval**.  
**Scope:** Reports how our user-accepted suit/C14 baselines, currently documented specialist equipment and licensed-RPG leads fit together under SWADE. **Every listed profile is at least a first-pass playable analog unless specifically flagged draft.** The whole cross-faction packet still requires Zerg/Protoss/vehicle opponents.

## Faction / source distinctions
- P1: Blizzard-original C-14 rifle, C-10 Ghost (variety of canisters), original Brood War UED Medic A-13, SCV engineering.
- P4: licensed *Alternity* **C-7 Stinger** and **CMC-405** model **not automatically StarCraft main canon**. 25mm C-10 specification is explicitly from P4, though original Blizzard confirms C-10 model.
- R: ordinary native SFC firearms, missiles, sensors, field healing and engineering.
- C: **Class II antimat gauss/penetrator rifle**, C-10 SWADE damage/range, A-13 stun implementation, and demo satchel mine analogy are *Savage StarCraft numerical conversions*. These are not fictional manufacturer product specs.

## Quick rules-interaction sanity checks
1. **Non-Heavy pistols, civilian rifles, shotguns and Light MGs** cannot defeat **Heavy Armor** merely because damage rolls are large. They remain threatening to civilian and unarmored characters.
2. **C-14** is unusual among ordinary infantry rifles because it has a provisional **Heavy Weapon** property and Class I. Equal-Class I Marine target: **1d3 Wounds per qualified hit**, before Soak and raises.
3. **Class II antimat penetrator** vs **Class II Marauder/Firebat**: **1d3 Wounds**; versus Class I Marine: **1d3+1**, not automatic instant kill of every character after Benny Soak.
4. **Class III SFC light rocket** vs Class III tank: **1d3 Wounds**; vs Class II heavy infantry: **1d3+1**; vs Class I Marine: **1d3+2**. Requires actual availability/launcher/ammo; every missile does not grant free extra attacks.
5. **C-10 Class I explosive SBT** supports Ghost assassination/control roles, but default damage may bounce off Class II suits. This is **intentional differentiation**; specific disable/lockdown effects require their own statted module.
6. **Medics:** Use printed SFC Medi-Gel/Scanner and Core Healing, not RTS instant Hit Point refills or licensed Alternity twice/day allowance.
7. **Tools:** SFC cutting torch/matter cutter are **Class II** for structural work, but close-range Improvised Weapon liabilities apply in combat.
8. **Grenade compatibility:** The SFC generic grenade launcher **p.60 excludes AP**; our **K12 Punisher AP** still requires the project-specific homebrew permission. New Ghost C-10 special ammo has its own compatibility rules, not unrestricted cross-caliber inventory.
9. **Suits:** Size 3 heavy suits have nine Mod slots; ranged armaments/foam/insulation must use their documented capacity. No suit has its own independent Wound pool or vehicle Out of Control check.
10. **Medic shield**: SFC **Personal Force Field +4 Toughness** is an option consistent with a protective shield concept but *not verified* to be the exact Brood War technology.

## Gaps to address after this *broad* batch
- **Zergling/Hydralisk/tough organism** normal vs Heavy Metal natural armor interactions, swarm and creep threat.
- **Protoss** Personal Force Field / psi-shield analogue, Zealot melee classes, Adept and ranged psionics.
- **Vehicle** Class III target(s) with fixed stats and field repair.
- **Stimulants** drug effect, benefit/cost/duration/overdose, plus application hardware.
- **Ghost electronics**: Lockdown, equipment compatibility and action economy.
- **Medic**: definitive shield vs licensed-derived light suit, field Restoration unit.
- **C-10 / A-13 / satchel** precise numbers are first-pass adaptations to compare only after cross-faction target profiles are ready.
