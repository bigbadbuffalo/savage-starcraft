**Heavy Metal check:** The proposed antimat rifle averages **2×6.5+8=21**, **Class II**, just over the boundary. Compared to Marine Class I it causes **1d3+1 Wounds**, compared to Firebat/Marauder Class II **1d3 Wounds**, per qualifying attack before Soak and raises. A standard SFC sniper has average **13+4=17**, **Class I**. An SFC light rocket is **6×3.5+16=37**, **Class III**: two classes over Marine I, one class over Firebat/Marauder II. See SFC p.93, and **normal character Soak** plus one suit Critical Hit per independently wounding attack, not vehicle maneuver rolls for worn suits.

## Ghost C-10 — role and source integrity
- **Blizzard's original [Ghost unit guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/ug.shtml)** identifies C-10 Canister Rifle, **variety of special munitions** and targeting/covert capabilities. The original *Alternity* PDF **p.107** says **25mm high-explosive** rounds. **Its exact 25mm specification is licensed-RPG provenance, not independently proved by the original Blizzard guide.**
- **Normal C-10 shell:** This conversion uses **3d6 Class I SBT at long rifle range** with Sniper-style Snapfire to model an explosive precision Ghost shot. These specific SWADE numeric values are **our provisional invention** combining SFC grenade/sniper precedents, **not** copied from the older RPG's Amazing/Good/Ordinary results.
- **Variable ammunition:** Smoke / EMP / Incendiary / Stun/flash *canister* rounds use the corresponding **SFC p.48 warhead quality when specific chamber-compatible 25mm canisters exist**, **not** the K12 magazine or automatic selection rules. Ammo logistics, availability, and exposure are GM-controlled. **Lockdown** is an explicitly Blizzard-supported Ghost effect, but standard SFC EMP doesn't reproduce an automatic 60s lockdown. An advanced **electronic incapacitation canister** can be designed later for the Ghost/psionics workstream. **Do not claim base EMP is full Lockdown**.
- **Target designator:** Ghost stealth/nuclear targeting supported by Blizzard. Do **not** turn a personal rifle's spotting laser into a free tactical nuclear strike; availability and actual ordnance authorization remain separate mission resources. The Ghost marks a target using ordinary line of sight/Notice/Electronics as appropriate, and artillery/nuclear intervention uses a separate action and permission.
- **Class boundary:** HE canister (I) is excellent against unarmored bodies via SBT rolled damage and can cause equal-Class wounds against Marine CMC I; it is weaker against heavier Class II suits without a specific qualified specialty payload. **A Ghost need not outgun a dedicated anti-materiel weapon**.

## Specialist loadouts and setting balance
- **Conventional anti-armor:** A purchased **Class II penetrator rifle** provides a one-shot specialist infantry response; the printed **Class III rocket** delivers genuine anti-vehicle escalation. Both are rare/encumbering; avoid letting any Marine casually carry ten rockets and a C-14 without Strength/Encumbrance consequences.
- **Squad rocket rationale:** Archon's licensed TMG Marine specialist upgrade depicts a rocket launcher, but its tabletop RNG cannot be converted into meters. The StarCraft RTS not depicting every team support weapon doesn't mean a mature Terran army cannot manufacture rockets.
- **Vehicle/human scaling:** SFC vehicles/mechs and Heavy Metal use published Class math. The **fixed suit Class** convention is separately owner-approved. Against **unclassed creatures and civilian bodies**, resolve normal rolled damage; don't treat a Zergling as a Class I vehicle for convenience.
- **No license laundering:** “Generic anti-materiel rifle” is **Savage StarCraft's descriptive noncanonical type**. Do not present `2d12/AP8` as a Blizzard C-10 or AGG-12 fact.
- **Open questions for integrated packet:** Exact C-10 range/area/round count, Ghost Lockdown device versus psionic ability, target designator action, rocket availability/reload and realistic antimat ammo mass.
