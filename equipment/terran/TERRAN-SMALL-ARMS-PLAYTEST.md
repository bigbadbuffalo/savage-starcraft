**Source precision:** StarCraft's 2000 Alternity PDF p.103 and p.111 describes **C-7 Stinger** as 8mm backup with minimal penetration. It gives **no literal SWADE metrics**. The **C-14's 8mm hypersonic penetrator is not mechanically equivalent** to another licensed-RPG 8mm pistol simply because caliber matches. Do not declare generic light/hunting guns to be heavy weapons for StarCraft infantry gameplay convenience.

## Use and limitations
- **AP is not HW**. If the target has Heavy Armor/Heavy Metal Class, first establish whether the weapon has **Heavy Weapon**; ordinary pistols and shotguns don't acquire penetration just by increasing AP. Against an unclassed/unarmored human or mundane beast use normal SWADE rolled damage/Toughness as usual.
- **C-7 / CMC support:** Sidearms remain useful when out of armor, in confined spaces, or when a carried C-14 is unavailable. With **Class II Marauder** or **CMC-660 Firebat**, an ordinary C-7 cannot function as a backup *anti-armor* response. That's expected.
- **SFC 3RB** (p.57): Generic assault rifle may fire a controlled three-round burst with its printed accuracy and damage benefit. **Don't equate that with every classed C-14 ability without referring to the separate C-14 draft**.
- **Do not model an `E-9` sidearm** solely from the line “4 clips of E-9 10mm ammo” (Alternity PDF p.79); the licensed source does not give a readable E-9 weapon description in that passage.
- **SFC Min Str** values are based on individual guns, not automatically increased/decreased when a Marine wears a Size 2 CMC-400; use normal equipped Strength. A carried heavy weapon never gains the SFC *Stabilizer* benefit unless actually **attached** to compatible powered armor.
