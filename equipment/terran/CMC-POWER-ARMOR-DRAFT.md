**Status:** **`playtest` provisional** for **CMC-300/400 Size 2 +10 Heavy/Class I; CMC-660 Size 3 +14 Heavy/Class II; Marauder 5-4 Size 3 +16 Heavy/Class II**. The Firebat/Marauder **Size 3 chassis choice** and equipment's **fixed Class rule** are approved; exact armor figures remain accepted provisional values. **No whole suit loadout is final/`approved`**. Firebat heat defense, installed weapon models, Medic alternatives and specialty equipment are still being built.  
**Date:** 2026-10-10.  
**Lore:** `lore/entries/terran/powered-combat-suits.md`; original SC98 manual p.35; *Stealing Thunder* (later P2 source, mirrored text partially read).  
**Rules:** Sci-Fi Companion Power Armor pp.119–127, Heavy Metal p.93; SWADE Core p.117.

## Common power armor chassis rules — current baseline
**CMC-300 and CMC-400** remain **Size 2**, as originally designed (SFC p.122): **+10 Heavy Armor baseline, Strength d12+1, Pace 6, six Mod slots**. **CMC-660 and 5-4 Marauder** now use the **owner-approved Size 3 chassis**: **+12 Heavy Armor baseline, Strength d12+2, Pace 4, nine Mod slots**, with selective Armor Mods to retain +14/+16 model ratings. Generic SFC unpowered weights/costs are 200 lb/$100K for Size 2 and 300 lb/$150K for Size 3; these are **not canonical StarCraft masses/prices**. All proper powered frames already grant HUD/optics, comms, enhanced hearing, environmental sealing, life support and three-day power (SFC pp.119–121). These require no extra Mods; the ordinary `Sealed` function is **not** the same as Self-Sealing against Wound breaches or enhanced fire-damage resistance.

Fixed Heavy Metal **Class** belongs to the **suit**, not the wearer's Vigor; permanently different protective redesigns can have their own Class. Character Wounds are Soaked normally. One **separately wounding attack** after Soak produces **one suit Critical Hit roll**, not one roll per Wound; separately wounding RoF attacks may cause multiple rolls. Vehicle Out-of-Control checks do not apply to an infantry wearer.

### What the ratings intentionally do
- Marine ↔ Marine: **Class I versus I** means ordinary C-14 hits can inflict **1d3 character Wounds**, potentially very lethal; published Soak and suit-critical-hit rules remain in force.
- Marine ↔ Firebat/Marauder: **Class I versus II** means ordinary C-14 hits cause **0**, raises **1 Wound**. They are harder to defeat with standard rifles, **not invulnerable**.
- Firebat vs Marauder: Both may be Class II, but are **not interchangeable**. Firebat's **heat/fire defense** is supported by original SC98 manual and must be built as an actual benefit rather than merely its Heavy Metal Class. Marauder's robust kinetic plating and arm-mounted **Punisher** ordnance are better supported by later fiction.
- A +1 Toughness Mod on the 5-4 affects ordinary SWADE Toughness against non-classed effects; it **does not dynamically push a fixed suit Class** to a different rating.
- **SFC Ballistic Protection** (p.123) reduces **slugthrower ordinary damage by 4**; under Heavy Metal's direct-Wounds procedure, there is no damage roll to subtract 4 from. **Don't buy this Mod as a magical anti-C-14 Class bump** without first verifying or explicitly extending it to Heavy Metal (which would be a new rule).
- **SFC Self-Sealing** (p.123) does **not** work when the wearer suffers **three or more Wounds from one attack**. Under lethal Heavy Metal fire, even well-maintained Marine armor can fail to maintain atmosphere after severe direct hits. Normal suit breach/Critical Hits apply.

## Medical protection — unresolved alternative
The original *Brood War* manual describes a UED Medic with medical equipment and protective shielding, not a definitive personal CMC rating. First test **+6 ordinary armor and shield/medical gear separately**, but do not assert that suit is environmentally sealed or powered merely because a Medic wears it. Alternate design: a **SFC Size 1 +8 Heavy Armor, Class I candidate** medical hardsuit with its own pace/Strength/Mods; requires more direct evidence and changes how threatening non-Heavy Weapons are. **Don't canonize an unverified CMC-405 model** from older RPG material.

## No hidden character class
Operators change suits if requisition/training/fitting allow it. A CMC-equipped soldier is not required to take a bespoke Marine Edge, and a non-Marine may wield a C-14 with the correct Strength/handling penalties.

## Firebat and Marauder role clarification — no baseline number change (2026-10-10)
The **original 1998 manual directly establishes heavier-than-Marine Firebat protection and specialized heat/flame insulation**. A *later Marauder design text* (currently verified only through secondary transcriptions) describes a roughly equal-weight CMC-660-derived 5-4 using **thicker neosteel in place of some insulation**. *Wings of Liberty* gives Firebats **Juggernaut Plating (+2 RTS Armor)** and Marauders **Kinetic Foam (+25 RTS HP)**, emphasizing distinct protective enhancements, not a simple ranked ladder. The licensed TMG currently supplies a **5-HP/4+ Armour Marauder** with **+1 HP Kinetic Foam** but **no released Firebat unit card yet** in checked public PDFs. **Keep baseline +14/Class II CMC-660 and +16/Class II 5-4**. Candidate specialty add-ons: enhanced **Juggernaut Firebat +16/Class II**, a dedicated heat/fire defense, and Marauder impact damping (cannot automatically be equated to +1 SWADE Toughness under Heavy Metal). Detailed comparisons: `../../development/benchmarks/FIREBAT-MARAUDER-ARMOR-COMPARISON.md`.

## Modifications compatibility note (2026-10-10; research only)
Primary-book investigation at `../../development/benchmarks/POWER-ARMOR-MODIFICATIONS-REVIEW.md` confirms the existing Mod table remains a **provisional suggestion, not a final slot allocation**. Notably: **Trauma Pack** works even after direct Heavy Metal Wounds by reducing penalties / Bleeding Out difficulty, though it does **not** protect against Wounds or duplicate Kinetic Foam physics; stock SFC **Assault Suit** is +16 Heavy/Class II and uses a Trauma Pack and grenade launcher. **Ballistic Protection** reduces rolled slugthrower damage rather than Heavy Metal Wounds, and **Energy Skin** reduces laser damage **only**. The Firebat's special immunity/resistance against direct flame-weapon attacks is **not covered by the suit's basic environmental sealing**; do not mislabel base Sealed as a full CMC-660 heat-defense rule. A plausible next Firebat slot budget is Armor x2 + Self-Sealing + Trauma Pack = **4/6**, keeping **2/6** for thermal/mount functions; removing Stabilizer is sensible unless its flamers actually benefit from attached direct-fire stabilization. Marauder could use Armor x3 + Trauma Pack = **4/6**, with Toughness/Targeting/launcher integration decided with complete weapon stats. **No numerical stats or Mod allocations are newly approved**.

## Kinetic Foam / Trauma Pack clarification — current proposal (2026-10-10)
**The prior proposed Trauma Pack was purely an RPG analogy and is withdrawn from the *recommended default* Firebat/Marauder builds pending explicit lore evidence for automatic medicinal injectors.** SFC Trauma Pack (p.124) administers drugs and is not an impact-cushioning gel system. The earlier Mod suggestions are retained above as dated design history, **not current preferred stock issue**.

**Kinetic Foam is a separate custom Mod candidate, not approved:** 1 Mod granting **+2 to ordinary Vigor Soak attempts against physical-impact Wounds** (not a free roll; still requires Bennies) would help against Heavy Metal where the printed **Toughness +1** and **Ballistic Protection –4 slugthrower rolled damage** do not. +1 Soak is a lighter balance variant. A baseline Marauder can use **Armor ×3 (3 Mods) + optional Kinetic Foam (1) = 4/6**, leaving 2 for K12 hardware and/or existing Toughness/Ballistic Protection. A Firebat can use **Armor ×2 (2) + optional Self-Sealing (1) = 3/6**, leaving 3 for thermal/delivery hardware. **Do not package Toughness, Ballistic Protection and Foam into one free or one-slot upgrade.** See `../../development/benchmarks/MARAUDER-KINETIC-FOAM-STUDY.md` for evidence and limitations; no item Armor/Class has changed.

## Approved equipment Mod — Kinetic Foam (2026-10-10)
**Owner decision:** **Kinetic Foam** is now an **approved optional Power Armor Modification** (1 Mod slot, maximum 1) granting **+2 on normal Vigor Soak rolls versus kinetic/concussive physical impacts**, including Heavy Metal, without a free Soak, second Wound track or change to Armor/Toughness/Class. Exclusions and current rules text: [Kinetic Foam](KINETIC-FOAM-MODIFICATION.md). **No default Trauma Pack** absent separate lore evidence.

**Foam-equipped Marauder prototype:** Size 2, **+16 Heavy Armor, fixed Class II; 3 Armor Mods + 1 Kinetic Foam = 4/6 Mod slots**. Remaining **2/6** slots may be used for K12 launcher systems and other purchased compatible Mods. This is a **foam-equipped loadout variant**, not proof that every 5-4 contains foam, and the rest of the provisional base stats are unchanged. Earlier research suggestions that call Kinetic Foam unapproved or give stock Pharma Trauma Pack remain recorded above for historical context but are superseded by this entry.

## Integrated weapons may favor Size 3 (2026-10-10; chassis change **not adopted**)
New detailed frame/weapon budget comparison: [Power Armor Frame Sizing Study](../../development/benchmarks/POWER-ARMOR-FRAME-SIZING-STUDY.md). The SFC **p.120 expressly allows vehicular weapons alongside power-armor Mods**; personal `Integrated Gear` is limited to 10 lb (p.125), while suitable **vehicular flamethrowers and grenade launchers require 1 Mod each, Min Size 1** (p.60). Hence Firebat's twin arm flamers or Marauder's twin K12 banks need **actual weapon Mod budget**, not free mounts; but **Size 2 is not forbidden**.

**Preferred new working candidate (not owner-approved):** Change **both CMC-660 and 5-4 together** from Size 2 (6 Mods, Pace 6) to **Size 3 (9 Mods, Pace 4)** while preserving **Firebat +14 Heavy/Class II and Marauder +16 Heavy/Class II**. That saves one Armor Mod per model and gains three inherent slots: **four extra available Mods at the same protective rating**. Size 3 +14 needs 1× Armor; Size 3 +16 needs 2× Armor. Counts with twin 1-Mod mounts, Firebat 1-Mod Self-Sealing and hypothetical thermal protection, or Marauder approved foam, leave **4/9 Mods** unspent in either case. The cost is slower default Pace, heavier unpowered suit, and increased cost; `Increased Pace` is a paid published option if needed. **Historical note superseded:** The owner **has now approved Size 3 for both models**, implemented in the current suit table at the top. Full K12 barrel/linking semantics and ammo fabricator are still open.

## Fire-resistant insulation option — new research candidate
The *Core Rules* **Environmental Resistance (Heat)** Ancestral Ability (p. 18) grants **+4 heat-resistance rolls and −4 damage from that source**. The *Environmental Protection* power (Core pp.160–161) expressly confirms heat protection can reduce **flamethrower damage**. SFC power armor defensive Mods (p.123) have **no fire-specific counterpart**: Energy Skin protects against *lasers only*. We therefore propose a **1-Mod equipment conversion**, [Fire-Resistant Insulation](FIRE-RESISTANT-INSULATION-DRAFT.md), **not yet owner-approved**. It handles rolled fire damage and heat hazard rolls, but **does not subtract Wounds assigned directly by Heavy Metal**. The Size 3 Firebat's 9-Mod capacity leaves room for this feature and integral weapons. This is a provisional component, not a new Ancestral Ability possessed by the wearer.

## First mounted weapon profiles — operational playtest package (2026-10-10)
**Twin Perdition flamethrowers** are now provisionally represented by **two linked SFC vehicular Heavy Flamethrowers**: **3d12 (I), Cone or MBT at 18, RoF1, Shots30 per arm, Incendiary, Heavy Weapon**, **+1 Shooting and +2 ordinary damage when linked**, costing **two weapon Mods**; see [Perdition](PERDITION-FLAMETHROWERS-PLAYTEST.md). The linked +2 rolled damage **does not increase Class above I** under SFC Heavy Metal.

**Two K12 Punisher arm assemblies** are provisionally represented by **two linked SFC vehicular grenade launchers**: **24/48/96, RoF3, 12 grenade Shots per arm, MBT, HW, Class I**, with **+1 Shooting and +2 normal damage when linked**. **Armor-piercing warheads** use SFC **p.48 3d4/AP8**, plus **p.93 reduction of target Class by one when Armor is 10+**. This provides real anti-Class-II ability using published mechanics. Optional **Concussive Shell** is a draft effect, not an approved second rule. See [Quad K12](QUAD-K12-PUNISHER-PLAYTEST.md). Weapon mounting uses vehicular Mods, **not** the 10-lb Integrated Gear limit.

**Current basic slot budgets:** Firebat **1 Armor +2 linked flame mounts +1 insulation +1 candidate Self-Sealing = 5/9**, leaving 4; Marauder **2 Armor +2 linked grenade mounts +1 optional approved Kinetic Foam = 5/9**, leaving 4; a **1-Mod Stabilizer** would give Marauder **6/9** and negate firing Recoil on attached direct-fire RoF3. Reload magazines and manufacturing gear cost additional Mods as appropriate. Older proposed weapon/mount loadouts in this research history are superseded by these detailed first weapon entries.
