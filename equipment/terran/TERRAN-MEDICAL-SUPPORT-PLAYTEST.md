# Terran Medical and Battlefield Support Equipment — First Pass

**Status:** `playtest` usable gear where a published SFC effect is directly stated; **draft** where a StarCraft-specific device or medical power needs design. 2026-10-10.  
**Primary Blizzard evidence:** Original *StarCraft: Brood War* manual printed pp. **10–11**, **UED Medic** (combat physician, **protective shielding**, field surgery, **A-13 Flash Grenade Launcher**, Optic Flare); [original Blizzard Medic guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/umedic.shtml) (Heal, Restoration, Optic Flare).  
**Licensed RPG comparison:** `../../lore/research/ALTERNITY-STARCRAFT-BOXED-SET-AUDIT.md`, scanned PDF pp. **103,111**: **CMC-405 Light Combat Suit**, C-7 Stinger sidearm, **Medical Kit with probes/surgical tools/heal-packs**. **This is NOT first-party evidence that Brood War UED Medics always wear a CMC-405 or carry the C-7**.

**Treatment rule:** Normal SWADE Core Healing, Golden Hour and Bleeding Out govern. The old Alternity folder allows an Alt RPG Medic to treat a patient twice/day, but **that does not automatically carry into SWADE**. Med-gel and Med-scanner grant their *printed* bonuses; don't add undocumented overlapping treatment-boost bonuses or infinite medpack charges.

## UED Medic protection — provide two valid playtest loadout concepts; do not fix lore prematurely

**A. Light combat Medic (licensed-RPG-inspired):** SFC **Combat Spacesuit +4* Armor and +2 Toughness**, sealed and self-sealing as printed on **p.43**, carrying scanner, supplies and optional C-7. This is a **mechanical analogue** for the Alternity light CMC-405, not a verified canonical UED suit. This armor is **ordinary Armor** (no fixed Heavy Metal Class), not Marine +10 Heavy Armor.

**B. Brood War shield Medic (stronger Blizzard-primary provenance):** An appropriately protective medical uniform/suit **and a distinct Personal Force Field** (SFC p.42, **+4 Toughness**, stacks with worn armor, separate battery/critical condition). **Not automatically combined with A's +2 Toughness without selecting a costed separate device**; the Brood War manual says "protective shielding" but does not define the mechanism as the SFC named Force Field. Both A/B are **candidates**, not owner choice.

**C-7 on a Medic** is supported by the **Alternity pregenerated character**, but the *Brood War* primary text identifies the A-13 as the Medic's sole **defensive armament**. Do not give *every Brood War UED Medic* a damaging gauss sidearm by default. The C-7 remains valid equipment for a different-era or personalized field doctor.

## A-13 Flash Grenade Launcher — first test

| Weapon | Range | Damage | RoF | Shots | Area | Traits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A-13 flash / optic flare projector** | **24/48/96** | **None** (photonic payload) | **1** | **6** | **Small Blast Template** | **Vigor −2** or **Stunned** for targets with exposed photo-optical perception; **−4 Vigor on attack raise** |

**Direct primary fiction:** Original Brood War manual pp.10–11 states A-13 releases an intense flash of white/ultraviolet illumination that damages organic retinas and optical sensors. **The SWADE effects above are a provisional conversion**, using **SFC p.48 Stun warhead** as the mechanical base but changing the delivery to **optical**. It does **not** deal normal damage, so no Heavy Metal Wound; protective opaque eye filters or non-visual targeting may negate the effect per fiction/GM; constructs with exposed optical sensors may be eligible even though a normal Stun warhead only affects unprotected beings. If the attack is dodged/misses or target cannot see the flash, no effect. **This is not P1 RAW or an approved universal blinding subsystem.** Full "permanent retinal damage" is **deliberately not imposed** on every use; a separate serious injury/lasting blindness rule would demand explicit testing.

## Restorative medical technology — preserve scope
Blizzard supports **nano-conveyed anesthesia and attenuated laser procedures** for Restoration, field surgery and removing adverse conditions. The SFC's **Medi-Gel/Scanner/Regeneration Gel** can represent varying capability levels. Don't automatically let every basic Healing kit purge psionic debuffs, Zerg infestations, poison, EMP or neural interference with one success: the RTS *Restoration* is a specialized medical intervention. Provisional procedure: for ordinary treatable poison/infection/injury, use **Healing** and relevant environmental/disease rules; for otherwise exotic afflictions, require an advanced device, appropriate diagnosis (Healing or Science), sufficient time and scenario-specific treatment (no universal flat target). Dedicated **Restoration tech module** remains a separate equipment/power choice.

## Stimpack — acknowledge source, don't invent pharmacology
- **Canon:** Terran Marine/Firebat stimulants exist in the RTS and source material.
- **Alternity PDF p.59** supply closet has **two combat stimulant doses**; does **not** provide SWADE effects.
- **Current project:** CMC-400's integrated stim *delivery hardware* can use a power-armor Mod, but the drug effects/duration/Fatigue/Wounds are **not yet settled**. Do not give an automatic gratis Athletics/Shooting damage boost by slotting a syringe into the suit.
- **Minimum usable packet:** Standard Med-Gel, Medi-Scanner, field surgical kit, light suit/shield candidates, and A-13. The future stim variant can be added when side effects are grounded and balance compared to other factions.

## Playtest roles
- **Field surgeon** without powered armor uses Healing/Notice/Science with scanner, chemistry and microtools; never needs a mandatory 'Medic class'.
- **SWADE Wild Card medic** can save Bennies for Soak as everyone else; Medical care does not produce a second healing/wound pool.
- **Battlefield UED medic** can spend actions tending the wounded and defending herself with flash equipment even when forbidden a damaging C-7 under primary source.
- **Hostile environment:** power suit sealed property or real combat spacesuit matters; a mere medical coat cannot survive hard vacuum.
