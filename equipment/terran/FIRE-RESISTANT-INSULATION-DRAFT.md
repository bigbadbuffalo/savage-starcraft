# Fire-Resistant Insulation — Power Armor Mod (Candidate)

**Status:** `draft` — an **unapproved custom Power Armor Modification** inspired directly by published SWADE Environmental Resistance (Heat). Proposed for the provisional **CMC-660 Firebat**, subject to owner decision and the integrated equipment packet.  
**Research date:** 2026-10-10.  
**Fiction:** The 1998 *StarCraft* manual, printed p. 35, expressly describes the CMC-660's additional heat/flame protection relative to ordinary Marine armor.  
**Published mechanical precedents:** *SWADE Core Rules* printed **p. 18**, *Environmental Resistance* positive Ancestral Ability (value **1 point**, not 1 Mod); Core **pp. 160–161**, *Environmental Protection* power (explicitly protects against flamethrower damage when its protection matches heat); *Science Fiction Companion* printed **p. 119** (already-Sealed heat protection), **p. 123** (defensive power armor Mods and laser-only Energy Skin).

## Candidate equipment rule (not yet approved)

**FIRE-RESISTANT INSULATION**  
**Mods:** **1 (maximum 1)**, proposed.  
**Cost:** TBD with Terran equipment economy.

Specialized insulating layers, reflective material, heat-resistant fuel plumbing, and active heat dispersion give the wearer **+4 on rolls to resist heat/fire environmental effects and hazards**, and **reduce damage from fire/heat sources by 4**, including direct fire attacks or flame-Trapping effects when conventional SWADE damage is rolled.

This is deliberately the **existing Environmental Resistance (Heat)** effect, transferred from *ancestry* to *worn, functional equipment*. It is **not** an additional ancestral trait of Terrans, and is not immunity to burning fuel, incendiary weapons, catastrophic heat, suit Critical Hits, or atmospheric-breach effects. It does not replace the Power Armor suit's already included environmental **Sealed** property (SFC p.119): ordinary CMC suits already regulate routine ambient temperature, but Firebats should resist **hostile fire attacks**.

**Heavy Metal limitation:** When a weapon instead uses SFC Heavy Metal **Class-based direct Wounds**, there is **no rolled damage total to subtract 4 from**. This proposed Mod does **not** alter Class or erase directly assigned Heavy Metal Wounds. It still helps on separate applicable fire/heat resistance rolls and conventional Fire damage. If significant unprotected fire-Wound cases remain once Perdition weapons are statted, evaluate a **separately specified conditional Soak rule** during integrated testing, but **do not silently grant it as part of this ancestry-derived Mod**.

**Equipment Mod accounting:** The Core ancestry trait costs **1 ancestral design point**, which is **not automatically equivalent** to 1 Power Armor Mod slot. The proposed **1 Mod** slot cost is an explicit **Savage StarCraft adaptation estimate**, defensible against the Companion's Energy Skin (-4 laser rolled damage, 0 Mod but vision Stealth cost) and protective options; revisit only in whole-packet balancing.

## Mechanic vs lore boundary
The **heat/fire resistance** is specifically established by Blizzard lore; the exact **+4 / -4** numbers are published SWADE **ancestry rules**, while assigning them as a purchased **Power Armor Modification** for an outfit is original conversion work. The source provides **no canonical numeric heat tolerance**, nor does the modified equipment rule automatically enable classed-flamer direct-Wound reduction.

**Next equipment tasks:** Determine whether Perdition weapons use the printed SFC personal/vehicular heavy flamers (with Heavy Metal Class I and Incendiary effects) or warrant specific properties, then assess whether the basic Firebat thermal resistance expresses the lore sufficiently without inventing a new damage subsystem.
