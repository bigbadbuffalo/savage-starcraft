# Kinetic Foam — Powered Armor Modification

**Game-design status:** **`approved`** as the Savage StarCraft custom Mod rule, by the project owner on 2026-10-10. Its effect is fixed for the current provisional equipment package; it may be revisited after integrated tests. **Not published SWADE RAW.**  
**Source:** *StarCraft II: Wings of Liberty* Armory's Kinetic Foam upgrade (currently checked via dated transcription of game text); see `../../development/benchmarks/MARAUDER-KINETIC-FOAM-STUDY.md`.  
**SWADE precedents:** *Science Fiction Companion* **Power Armor Modification slots**, printed pp. 122–125; SWADE Core **Soak** p. 96, **Iron Jaw** p. 42.

## Rules text — owner approved

**KINETIC FOAM**  
**Mods:** 1 (maximum 1).  
**Cost:** To be established with the rest of the Terran equipment economy.

A fitted shock-dispersion undersuit grants the wearer **+2 to Vigor Soak rolls against Wounds caused by kinetic or concussive physical impacts**—such as bullets or gauss spikes, blunt-force blows, collision or falling impacts, and the concussive force of explosions.

This Mod **does not grant a free Soak roll**. The wearer must be able to Soak normally and spends a Benny or other normally permitted resource as usual. The +2 applies to that ordinary Soak roll, including when Heavy Metal determines the Wounds. **Do not modify Armor, Toughness, Weapon Class, suit Class, or the amount of Wounds before the Soak roll.** The usual suit breach and Critical Hit rules still apply to any Wounds remaining after Soak.

The bonus **does not apply** to fire or heat, energy beams, electrical effects, acid or poison, radiation, psionic harm, or any other damage **without a qualifying physical impact**. For mixed-effect attacks, apply it only if the Wounds being Soaked are attributable to the kinetic/concussive impact rather than assuming all explosives or ammunition effects qualify.

## Equipment and scope
- **One** Mod slot on any compatible powered armor suit. This is a portable equipment rule, **not** an Edge, class feature, or general bonus for all Marauders.
- The fictional basis is the **Marauder Kinetic Foam upgrade**, **not proof that every 5-4 suit in every era carries it by default**. The working basic Marauder profile is still **+16 Heavy Armor, fixed Class II**. The **foam-equipped Marauder variant now uses an approved Size 3 chassis**: **Armor ×2 (2 slots) + Kinetic Foam (1 slot) = 3/9 slots** before K12 attachments and other Mods; with two provisional 1-Mod K12 mounts this would be **5/9 slots**. The previously researched Size 2/4-of-6 budget is historical, not current.
- **Trauma Pack is not included** merely because a stock SFC Assault Suit uses one; pharmaceuticals are not equivalent to impact-damping foam.
- Published **Toughness +1** and **Ballistic Protection** remain separate optional Mods, with their normal slot costs. Neither automatically duplicates this Soak effect under Heavy Metal.
- The +2 is a bonus to an ordinary Soak roll, so it follows the rules' usual treatment of modifiers; **no free roll, second Soak track, blanket Wound reduction or special stacking exception** is introduced.
- **Extra NPC caveat:** ordinary Extras usually Soak only if an appropriate Benny is spent. This Mod does not independently make them eligible for a free Soak. Our pre-existing Class II already protects an Extra Marauder from ordinary Class I rifle hits.

## Source/interpretation boundary
- **Lore:** foam protects the wearer from forces transmitted through the shell (Wings of Liberty description; full first-party text verification remains outstanding).
- **Published mechanics:** Mod slot framework, Vigor Soak rolls, and precedent for a +2 Soak modifier from Iron Jaw.
- **Original adaptation:** making that bonus **equipment-granted, impact-limited and one Mod slot** is **Savage StarCraft approved homebrew**. Do not cite it as a Pinnacle power-armor modification.

**Balance state:** Rule approved, but cost, default procurement, exact suit mount allocation, and measured performance are still to be resolved with the **integrated playtest packet**, not as an isolated design gate.
