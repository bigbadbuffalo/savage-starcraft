# Terran Engineering, Reconnaissance, Field Logistics and Demolitions — First Pass

**Status:** `playtest` for native SWADE gear and explicit adaptation candidates; **not** a claim that every item is a named Blizzard production model. 2026-10-10.  
**Primary Blizzard scope:** Original [SCV guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/Terran/uscv.shtml) (construction, mineral handling, repair, vacuum capability); original [Ghost guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/ug.shtml) (stealth, sensing, target coordination); original 1998 Marine manual (CMC systems).  
**Licensed comparison:** `../../lore/research/ALTERNITY-STARCRAFT-BOXED-SET-AUDIT.md`: data pad, medical diagnostics, specialized cloak, CMC-405, 10kg satchels, mini-computers and ammunition stores in PDF pp. **59, 79, 107, 111**. Specific magazine counts and the 10-round cloak recharge are **Alternity** figures, not universal StarCraft engineering facts.

## Demolition and sabotage — usable field prototype

**Alternity anchor:** PDF p.107 gives a Ghost-character **Demolitions Kit with two 10kg satchel charges**; the source specifies no SWADE explosive damage or Armor Class. It also depicts improvised sabotage missions, electronic security and remote devices.

**Adaptation:** A compact **field demolition satchel** is a *placed explosive* mechanically based on the **SFC p.62 Mine**: **4d6 damage, AP10, Heavy Weapon/Class II, Large Blast Template**, one use. Use a **prepared charge**, not a hand-thrown grenade. A qualified operator uses **Repair** to prepare a structural breach, **Electronics** for digital remote timer/switch, or **Thievery** for trap concealment/defusal, with ordinary SWADE situational modifiers. Placement takes meaningful time determined by target and conditions; for a quick encounter use **one minute in a noncombat scene** as the starting benchmark, and require a specific action if triggering in combat. Triggers must be set; do not assume every satchel has AI friend-or-foe IFF.

**Critical caveat:** The **SFC Mine is 5 lb**, whereas each Alternity charge is described as **10 kg**. **We are borrowing a fair playtest blast template and Class II baseline, NOT claiming these two objects have identical mass/yield.** The 10kg item can be roleplayed as a bulky engineering charge, and the game effects can be adjusted after a proper structural-destruction / blast-vs-heavy-armor benchmark. The weapon's listed Class II already threatens Class II armor; no need to force a new Class IV to imitate the old Alternity 2d8/wound table. **Demolitions kit** includes gloves, tampers, digital detonator/anti-tamper tools; no automatic Repair/Thievery bonus merely for possession.

**Alternative:** Use the printed **SFC Mine** unchanged at weight5, 4d6/AP10/Class II, **LBT**, with its p.62 costs and sensor-trigger options, rather than pretending every device is a 10kg satchel. For explosive warhead modifications use the actual SFC **p.48 / p.62** allowed types; do not silently add K12 Punisher's custom AP-grenade compatibility to other ordnance.

## Ghost cloak / civilian stealth equipment

**Blizzard original Ghost guide:** Ghost suits have personal stealth and sensors used for nuclear target coordination.  
**Alternity PDF p.107:** The Ghost's **Hostile Environment Suit** includes communications/NBC, light small-arms protection and a cloak **lasting ten Alternity combat rounds before charging**. These duration numbers belong only to the licensed **Alt** character.
**SFC equipment solution:** Suit **Cloaking Skin** (p.42) is activated `invisibility` with native SFC battery/duration conventions; SFC **Power Armor Stealth System** (p.124) is a **different, explicitly moddable system**, subject to chassis size and stealth mode's limitations. Use **one** chosen mode, not a free combination of every +2/-4 modifier. A fully enveloping suit/environmental life-support outfit may carry cloaking components as a **specialized paid item**; define exact armor/weight/price in Ghost package rather than assuming any civilian lab coat has it.

## Three initial field mission kits (gear checklist, not arbitrary stat boosts)
1. **Colonial survey technician:** Hand scanner, personal data device, binoculars, commlink, sealed protective clothing/respirator when necessary, cutting torch, repair tools, adhesive patches.
2. **Combat engineer/demolitions trooper:** CMC-compatible comms, motion detector, repair/lock tools, cutting tool, demolition kit and 1–2 charges, optional specialist Class II antimat weapon.
3. **Scout/Ghost infiltration:** Compact C-10 canister system, stealth suit, commlink, datapad, demolition kit, scanner/optics, one specialty canister; *nuclear target designator* only when mission/command actually supplies a strike.

## Source boundaries and future work
- The **SCV** is widely established in Blizzard game lore. **No dedicated T-280 armored walker stats** are established here; a later mechanized-equipment workstream will supply that vehicle/power-loader profile.
- **Electronic locks** and field gear are equipment; the adventure scenarios' door puzzles do not establish industrial security tolerances for all Terran worlds.
- The source shows the existence of **combat stimulants**, but the actual drug pharmacology and SWADE effect are deferred deliberately in `TERRAN-MEDICAL-SUPPORT-PLAYTEST.md`. Do not give untested 'stimpacks' infinite bonuses.
- **SFC generic prices** are not real StarCraft credits: they are useful relative placeholders, and faction supply/rarity is the GM's choice.
