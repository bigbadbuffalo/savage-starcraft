# Twin Perdition Flamethrowers — Terran Firebat

**Status:** `playtest` **equipment prototype**, not final numeric approval and not a claim of canonical weapon ranges/fuel capacity. Authored 2026-10-10 under the user's *build the complete packet first* policy.  
**Canonical anchor:** SC98-MANUAL printed **p.35**; Blizzard's original [Firebat unit guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/ufire.shtml) expressly describes **two arm-mounted Perdition flamethrowers**, close-assault employment, and a more heat-resistant suit. **No quantified fuel tank capacity or real-world range is given**. Later accounts of **combusto-plasma** fuel are indexed in secondary [Perdition](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/Perdition_flamethrower) coverage; physical construction details require firsthand source inspection.  
**Rules:** *Science Fiction Companion* printed **p.60** (*vehicular Heavy Flamethrower*), **pp.44–45** (*Linked Weapons, Incendiary, Cauterize*), **p.52** (*personal flame weapons may be Evaded; plasma option*), **p.93** (*Heavy Metal*), **p.120** (*Power Armor may install vehicular weapons*). *SWADE Core* printed **p.97** (Cone and area effect/raises), **p.100** (Evasion), **p.127** (fire/ignition).

**These are published vehicular weapon values and SFC linked-weapon modifiers put into a Firebat-shaped equipment package**. The linked pair is **one attack**, not two independent RoF actions or two Cone attacks for free. One fuel unit per projector is consumed when the pair fires together. **Fire one arm alone** to consume fuel from only that projector and forego the linked +1/+2. Shots 30 is borrowed from the SFC vehicular weapon's ammunition figure, **not** a canon statement about the CMC-660's tank.

**Installation:** The SFC vehicular Heavy Flamethrower is **1 Mod, Min Size 1**. Two properly integrated forearm mounts consume **2 Mods on a Size 3 suit**. Don't silently charge the 10-lb *Integrated Gear* limit instead; this is a vehicular weapon installation under SFC p.120. SFC p.45 explicitly prices each additional **Linked** weapon at **+1 Mod**, which is satisfied by the two-mount budget; don't double-charge a third slot for the same second weapon.

## Shooting, templates, ignition and cover
1. **Cone** extends **9 inches (18 yards)** on the tabletop, per Core p.97; alternatively use **Medium Blast Template (MBT) at 18 inches** with the printed SFC vehicle flamer. Those are tabletop ranges; not engineering maximums.
2. One Shooting attack affects those in the chosen template under the standard area-effect rules. A **raise** adds normal damage dice as allowed; those aware of a flame attack may **Evade** according to the published flamer precedent (SFC p.52; Core p.100). Do **not** multiply the usual raised damage by two for using two linked projectors.
3. Flamer is **Incendiary**: individuals may ignite under Core p.127; ongoing fire damage is a separate hazard. Targets partially concealed by cover use the core area-effect/cover rules (Core pp.97–98).
4. The default CMC-660's **Sealed** property and **provisionally accepted Fire-Resistant Insulation** help its wearer withstand atmosphere, heat/fire resistance rolls and **−4 rolled** fire damage. They do **not** nullify another Firebat's attack and don't automatically make friends in its cone fireproof.
5. SFC's **optional plasma-filled flamer** (pp.52,60) is a **technology/ammunition variant to consider** if later lore confirms the specific Firebat model's combusto-plasma design. Its published normal-damage benefit on an attack raise does **not** automatically trigger SFC p.93's **Plasma *warhead*** Heavy Metal +2-Wound effect: a flamer is **not a fitted plasma warhead**.

## Heavy Metal and cross-equipment consequences

Average **3d12 = 19.5**, giving **Weapon Class I** with AP0. For the **linked** pair, SFC p.93 expressly excludes **linked damage bonuses** from Class calculation: it remains **Class I**, even though normal rolled damage averages **21.5**. This prevents twin flamers from becoming anti-tank simply because their streams overlap.

| Target | Heavy Metal rule output for a qualifying hit | Firebat significance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Class I Marine CMC** | **1d3 direct Wounds**, plus +1 if attack is with raise | Firebat close-range flame can be deadly to peer infantry |
| **Class II Marauder or Firebat** | **0 direct Wounds**, plus **1 on an attack raise** | Heavy armor seriously resists the Class I flame discharge; secondary ignition, breach, or called-shot consequences may still matter |
| **Class III+ tank** | **No Effect** from the base Class I flame | Flamethrowers are not default anti-tank hardware |
| **Unclassed Zerg, unarmored targets** | Normal **3d12 / linked 3d12+2** damage, fire and Evasion procedures | **Do not** give Zergling/other biological tissue Heavy Metal Class merely for having natural carapace |

**Open interaction:** With fixed-Class Heavy Metal, a Firebat's extra flame resistance reduces **ordinary rolled fire damage**, but there is no damage roll to reduce with a Class I-versus-Class II direct-Wound result. It still protects from environmental fire and burning. Do **not** invent a -1 Wound or immunity vs classed flame hits as an unapproved add-on; revisit only if whole-packet tests expose a major mismatch.

## Why this version first?
Published weapon+link mechanics cover short-range anti-organic area fire, a heavy-weapon tag for eligibility against sealed Heavy Armor, and arm integration with **two slots**, without a brand-new damage subsystem. **Main uncertainty:** is the stock Class I vehicular flamer *too ineffective* against Class II heavy suits once ignition and cover are considered? Keep as-is until Zerg/Protoss/combined-arm weapon ecology exists; one source-story incident doesn't prove every thermal blast should breach armored Marauders.
