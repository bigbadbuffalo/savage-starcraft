# Protoss Psi-Blades and Ranged Ground Weapons — First Mechanical Ladder

**Status:** `playtest` candidate conversion, **2026-10-11**. No user approval for individual numbers. Published SWADE mechanical anchors are noted separately from explicitly **custom StarCraft equipment**.  
**Primary evidence:** [Zealot primary lore](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/units/zealot.shtml): suit-powered psi blades and armored-structure cutting; [Dark Templar](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/units/darktemplar.shtml): shadow-assassin blade; [Dragoon](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/units/dragoon.shtml): psi-charged antiproton disintegration bolts; [SC2 Stalker balance/role](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/20975163/starcraft-ii-multiplayer-major-design-changes): particle disruptor anti-armor; [Adept](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/18291111/legacy-of-the-void-beta-preview): glaive cannon, teleporting Shade.
**Rules:** *Science Fiction Companion* printed **p.50** Psi Blade / Energy Sword, **p.46 Psychic quality**, **p.93 Heavy Metal**, Core **p.170 Teleport**, basic Core powers.

**SFC Psychic quality:** The published SFC **p.46** weapon channel lets an **Arcane Background (Psionics)** wielder spend 1–3 PP **before firing/striking** for +2 rolled damage per point. Baseline weapon activation through the Zealot's power harness is **not itself a free grant of AB Psionics**. Under **Heavy Metal**, the project records each weapon's Class as its **printed/specifically calculated equipment line**; **do not automatically upgrade weapon Class because temporary +2 psychic attack damage was added**, any more than we count linked weapon damage for Class. A special *overloaded blade mode that raises Class* would be a separate priced/powered ability with explicit balance testing.

**Armor interaction:** A Class II Zealot blade vs a Class I Marine causes **1d3+1 direct Wounds** (+1 on Fighting raise) before Soak; vs Firebat/Marauder II causes **1d3**; vs proposed Ultralisk Class III usually **0 Wounds, 1 on attack raise**. Heavy Metal conversion is lethally sensitive to Gang Up, Fighting, and multiple attacks; **two physical blades are not automatically two free attacks**. Two-Fisted/ambidexterity or multiple-action rules apply as usual.

**Natural vs mechanical:** Dragoons are physically **walkers** with severely wounded Protoss interfaces; Stalkers are **quadrupedal mechanical combat platforms**. They are not automatically common Protoss wearing CMC-like suits. Their ranged weapons are **equipment** with defined classes, not innate species-telekinetic bolts.

**Test priority:** The huge difference between **SFC base Psi Blade Class I** and **custom enhanced Class II** is intentional. Run Marine/FIrebat/Marauder/Ultralisk vs Zealot fights before deciding if *every* zealot blade merits Class II. A Class II psi blade may be extremely lethal to a suit wearer; cover/closing distance and +4 Toughness shield remain material defenses for Zealots.
