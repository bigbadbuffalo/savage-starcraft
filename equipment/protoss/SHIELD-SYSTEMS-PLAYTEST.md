# Protoss Personal Shields — Two Native SWADE Starting Models

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Status:** **Candidates** for integrated `playtest`; **NOT owner-selected shield rules**.  
**Primary lore:** Blizzard [original Protoss shield overview](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/bstrat.shtml) makes **plasma shields a defensive energy layer** on nearly all military units and robotic machines; absorbs kinetic and energy hits, **depletes**, and **recharges** over time / at a battery. Independent [Zealot](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/units/zealot.shtml) description calls out armor that channels psionic energy to form protection.  
**Published SWADE sources:** *Science Fiction Companion* printed **p.42** Personal Force Field, **p.123** power armor Shields Modification; Heavy Metal **p.93**, Core regular Toughness/Armor and Soak.

## Candidate A — closest zero-homebrew equipment analog (recommended first comparison)

**Personal Plasma Field (SFC Personal Force Field analogue)**  
**Physical item:** one personal **Force Field, Y, SFC p.42** integrated into a Protoss harness/armor. **Uses the published Personal Force Field effect without modification**:
- +**4 Toughness**, stacking with worn **physical** armor (but not with another worn armor's Toughness as excluded by normal SFC stacking). Treat as **Toughness**, not +4 Armor or +4 Heavy Armor.
- Protected Size **2 or less**, on/off **limited free action**.
- If the wearer suffers **three or more Wounds from one hit**, the battery is exhausted and needs recharging. **The printed device does NOT continuously deplete on regular hits, doesn't supply extra Wound capacity, and has no defined standard automatic per-round recharge.**
- Any physical armor retained on the wearer is a **separate equipment item**. The field does **not** make ordinary worn armor into Heavy Armor or give it a Heavy Metal Class.
- **Interaction:** Against C-14 Class I heavy weapon **when the Zealot's own plating is *unclassed ordinary Armor***, resolve **normal 2d10 rolled damage**, AP4 vs physical armor, and +4 Toughness from field helps. Against an attack using **Heavy Metal's Class-Wound pathway on a different Classed armored wearer**, +4 Toughness is **not** an automatic direct-Wound reduction.
- **Lore compromise:** shields fictionally recharge/deplete in layers before armor; this PFF doesn't really do that for lesser hits. Treat it as a **minimal action-light RPG approximation**, not exact replication.

## Candidate B — charge-based **modified equipment adaptation** (not standard SFC Personal Force Field RAW)

**Protoss Shield Emitter (SFC p.123 Shields conversion)**  
**Working hypothesis:** A standalone or worn-harness variant of the published **Power Armor Shields** Mod, **not** pretending it is the SFC p.42 Force Field:
- Pilot/wearer has **2 provisional field charges** (not derived from TMG's printed Shield value). On taking Wounds, the generator **automatically spends a charge for an Electronics-based Soak** as a free action, and under SFC p.123 it **must continue expending charges until all incoming Wounds are negated or its charges run out**. This is the actual printed power-armor Shields consumption rule, not a voluntary single-charge retry.
- Recharges all charges after **10 minutes outside combat** (SFC p.123), with in-combat emergency recharge **deferred**: that printed emergency option diverts suit locomotive and weapons power, and **a generic wearable emitter may not have identical shared power systems**. A genuine powered Zealot harness could integrate the original emergency rule **if its power coupling is actually specified**.
- The shield applies even when damage came from Class-based Heavy Metal, as regular Soak does, **and** to unclassed rolled-damage Wounds.
- **Does NOT include Candidate A's +4 Toughness** unless explicitly purchased/designed as a separate effect. This matters greatly for balance.
- **Technical gap:** SFC Shields is a Mod **only on power armor** with charges based on the suit's Size. A separate Protoss armor harness has **no selected SFC power armor Size**. **Choosing 2 charges, a hand-worn device and Electronics d8 without an installed SFC frame are original conversion guesses.** Do not claim B is printed RAW.
- **Soak interaction to adjudicate:** The SFC p.123 field's **mandatory shield Electronics-Soak sequence is distinct from an optional character Benny Soak**, but the precise priority and whether any subsequent wearer Soak is eligible after failed shield charges must be adjudicated against the Core one-Soak-per-attack principle. **Do not grant a free additional biological Soak** just by stacking two different systems; explicitly test this before a default shield rule is approved. Field charges spent as the published Mod requires.
- **Lore strength:** Better depletion, regeneration, and protection from classed weapon hits; increased bookkeeping compared with +4 Toughness.

## Candidate C — merged bespoke system **intentionally rejected at this stage**
**+4 Toughness plus 2 free-charge Electronics Soaks, automatic regeneration and other penalties stacked for every Zealot** would be significantly stronger than either published effect alone, and could trivialize normal rolled C-14 fire and Class I/II attacks. **Not an approved Protoss feature.**

## Technology/context details
- **Sentry placed Force Field** is a **battlefield barrier** (Blizzard [SC2 Force Field guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/5963109/game-guide-protoss-force-field)), distinct from the personal shields on all Protoss military units. Model by **Core Barrier** or SFC **Force Field device**, not p.42 personal protective Toughness.
- **Guardian Shield** provides ranged-fire protection to nearby troops, not energy-field battery recharging. Compare Core **Protection/Deflection** powers on allied units or specialized device **support activation**, no free overlapping stacked all-party +4 field.
- **Shield Battery** is a distinct external recharging installation, not every Zealot's normal emergency free action.
- **EMP:** Blizzard's original Zealot guide identifies the Science Vessel EMP Shockwave as a shield counter. Candidate A could disable the emitter when electronics are hit; Candidate B might consume/discharge charges. Exact EMP-vs-shield interface needs owner decision; don't silently zero any allied psionic PP just because shields fail.

**Test scenarios:** Marine C-14 RoF3 shoot a shielded Zealot (Armor+6 ordinary; Vigor d10; +4 Toughness model A) and compare with model B 2 charges; follow with Hydralisk needle spam, Zergling melee, Marauder Concussive AP, and an Ultralisk blade. Determine whether ordinary C-14 AP4 bypasses too much of the worn physical Armor, shield refresh cadence, role of cover/charging speed, who rolls Electronics for a psionically controlled field and whether Wild Card vs Extra creates disproportionate survival. Only **then** select default A/B or a narrower adaptation.
