# Equipment Catalog

**State:** First numerical draft prototypes created; **no item statistics approved or session-playtested**. Approved underlying Setting Rules remain separate from item profiles.

Separate equipment from character classes. A Marine may wield specialist weapons; Firebat/Marauder/Ghost labels describe occupational roles and usual loadouts, not immutable equipment restrictions. Track availability, compatible physiology, ammunition or power supply, training and faction logistics only where relevant.

First benchmarks: C-14-type infantry rifle (lore research first), specialist anti-armor launcher, CMC power armor, heavy infantry gear, field medical and demolition kits.

Use existing SWADE/Sci-Fi Companion equipment where possible. Adopted values must preserve meaningful differences among unarmored, powered infantry, Heavy Armor and warship targets.
