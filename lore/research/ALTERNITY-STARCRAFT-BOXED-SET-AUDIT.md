# Alternity: StarCraft Edition (2000) — Licensed RPG Equipment Audit

**Date inspected:** 2026-10-10  
**Status:** **Source review complete for the uploaded 134-page scanned boxed-set compilation**; **not** a comprehensive inventory of every Alternity rulebook or fan expansion.  
**Source ID:** `SC-ALT2000-BOX`; Wizards of the Coast / Blizzard-marked *Alternity Adventure Game: StarCraft Edition*, by Shawn F. Carnes, David Eckelberry, and Bill Slavicsek, copyright 2000. **Licensed derivative/source tier P4** for invented equipment and RPG mechanics; its reappearance of Blizzard names is cross-checked separately.  
**Private source:** User-uploaded `259959459-Alternity-StarCraft-Boxed-Set.pdf`. **Do not commit or redistribute full PDF**. **Important:** image-only scan; citations below use **1-based PDF page** and original printed page/section when present; the combined scan contains Rules Book, Adventures Book, six+ character folders, and screen, so a printed "p. 7" alone would be ambiguous.

**Document structure matters:** The *Rules Book* occupies PDF pp. **2–49**; *Adventures Book* pp. **50–97**; individual pregenerated character folders pp. **98–129**, followed by GM screen **130–133** and back cover. These are not a full *StarDrive Arms & Equipment Guide*, and this upload does **not** contain the separate community-written Alternity expansion documents found online. No basis to ascribe the internet's fan supplement weapons to this licensed box.

## Cross-source checks
- Blizzard's original official [Ghost guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/ug.shtml) **directly** identifies the **C-10 Canister Rifle**, variable special ammunition and Ghost targeting/stealth role. It does **not independently specify the Alternity 25mm value** in that guide.
- *StarCraft: Brood War* original manual printed **pp.10–11** identifies the **Medic** as support physician with protective shielding and the **A-13 Flash Grenade Launcher** used for Optic Flare; reproduced original manual: https://manuals.plus/m/427fe339417b94f7ac935269b64be58544d55d49ab7ba1e90cf2552db0fd80bc.pdf . **CMC-405 is not established by that primary passage**.
- Blizzard's [original Medic guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/umedic.shtml) confirms combat healing, restorative nanomedical/laser techniques, and optical flash. Its in-game instantaneous HP/energy budgets **aren't automatically SWADE healing rates**.
- The old licensed RPG invents some terminology and game details. A [community StarCraft Wiki entry](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/CMC-405_light_combat_suit) also flags **CMC-405 as RPG-only / not regarded as canon**. This is a useful finding aid, but our controlling reason is source provenance itself.
- Publication described as a **Wizards of the Coast 2000 licensed fast-play game**, not full 300-page Alternity gear compendium. External bibliographic support https://www.rpg.net/reviews/view-printable.phtml?reviewNumber=6651 ; user upload copyright p.102 (PDF index p.102) and front credits p.3.

## Adopt / adapt / exclude
1. **Adopt as content inspiration:** C-7 backup gauss pistol, versatile C-10 Ghost canister system, CMC-405 *optional licensed-derived* support suit, navigation and diagnostics tools, Ghost cloaking suit, satchels, stimulants, field supply lists. Keep clear *licensed RPG lead* tags on non-Blizzard names.
2. **Use published SWADE gear before inventing rules:** SFC **p.34** Commlink/Biolink/Cutting Torch, **p.35** Motion Detector/Matter Cutter, **p.37** Personal Data Device/Scanner, **p.39** Medi-Gel/Medi-Scanner, **pp.42–43** seals and body armor, **pp.53,56–57** launchers, rifles, machine guns and shotguns, **p.93** Heavy Metal.
3. **Do not copy the Alternity 'Amazing/Good/Ordinary' damages, action phases, magazine counts, daily treatment limits or −1/+1 steps.** They are not SWADE stats and were produced for a small introductory game.
4. **No dubious canon promotion:** The C-7 and CMC-405 are **licensed derivative additions** unless independently corroborated. The C-10 is also **Blizzard-supported** (with 25mm RPG-specific).
5. **No full PDF in repo**. This audit is an original abstract with traceable page indices.
