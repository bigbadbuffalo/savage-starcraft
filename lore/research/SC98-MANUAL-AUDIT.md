## Important epistemic warning
The manual combines expository fiction, in-universe understanding, recognizable military classifications, and material describing **game systems**. The existence of mobile buildings, Creep, shields and other equipment is lore-relevant, but production queues, upgrade progression, unit hit points and balance constraints are *not* automatically canonical physical limits. The Protoss history introduction explicitly attributes very ancient events to fragmentary records and traditions (p. 71); treat such claims as in-universe historiography when later evidence differs.

## A. Terran findings: exiles, governments and flexible military

| Claim | Direct manual evidence | RPG importance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T-M01 | pp. 26–28: UPL purges, Doran Routhe's exile plan, ATLAS genetic screening and early psionic potential among a small group of prisoners. | Terran psionics have a human hereditary/background dimension; the colony founding is morally and politically complex. |
| T-M02 | pp. 28–30: the exiles' ships disperse to Umoja, Moria and Tarsonis; the colonies later develop independent political and economic systems. | Terrans are not a unitary culture; starting location/faction matters more than choosing a generic “Terran class.” |
| T-M03 | pp. 30–31: Tarsonian-led Confederacy, the Kel-Morian Combine, Guild Wars and Umojan Protectorate. | Corporate security, smuggling, competing militaries, espionage, resource disputes, frontier authorities. |
| T-M04 | pp. 31–33: Korhal repression and annihilation, the Sons of Korhal and mounting alien incursions. | Political rebellion and wartime loyalties coexist with alien-horror campaigns. |
| T-M05 | p. 34: Terran success rests on adaptation, mixed troop types, modular/mobile facilities and continuing food/fuel/spare-parts needs. | Logistics matter in fiction; no need to simulate RTS supply population or construct a separate building mini-game. |
| T-M06 | p. 35: Confederate Marine profile describes resocialized recruits, protective life-support/NBC combat armor and the 8 mm C-14 Impaler weapon. | **A standard issue loadout, not proof that every Marine everywhere is always armed identically.** Armour and rifle require separate catalog items. |
| T-M07 | p. 35: Firebat profile differentiates a heavier heat-protective combat suit and specialized flamethrowers; p. 36 describes the chemical stim delivery system and adverse effects. | Role chiefly equipment/training and optional chemical system; a Firebat character class is unnecessary. |
| T-M08 | pp. 36–37: Ghosts are born with psionic aptitude, trained from childhood, fitted with dampeners; suit-based cloaking can be absent in older gear, while special ammunition can disable machinery. | Keep *psionic aptitude*, *conditioning*, *implant*, *cloak device*, *C-10 ammunition* distinct. |
| T-M09 | pp. 37–38: Goliath is a walker with twin 30 mm autocannons and Hellfire anti-air missiles; Arclite tank has a mobile gun configuration and an artillery mode. | Independent vehicle/weapon/crew profiles. Weapons for different targets should matter. |
| T-M10 | pp. 39–40: Wraith fighter variants and Behemoth-class battlecruiser are distinguished by weapons, roles and armor; Behemoth uses multiple batteries and heavy NeoSteel armor. | Do not project infantry-vs-battlecruiser RTS damage relationships into tabletop combat. |
| T-M11 | pp. 48–49: Umojan Protectorate is politically autonomous; Kel-Morian Combine involves armed mining interests with complex relations to the Confederacy. | Different Terran equipment access and mission sponsors; faction political material must be time-scoped. |

**Inferences for campaign design (not direct manual assertions):** A party of colonial law officers, technicians, mercenaries, medical personnel and soldiers can credibly cooperate. A modular loadout system reflects the text better than RTS-unit classes. Pre-Dominion Confederate recruit practices should **not** be assumed universal across all later Terran forces.

## B. Zerg findings: biology as infrastructure and warfighting

| Claim | Direct manual evidence | RPG importance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Z-M01 | pp. 51–52: manual narrates early genetic experimentation on Zerus, biological assimilation and deliberately evolved adaptive strains. | Creature profiles derive from physiology and particular lineage; avoid one generic Zerg ancestry for all organisms. |
| Z-M02 | p. 52: the Overmind commands via specialized Cerebrates, which direct subordinate Queens and Overlords. | Command nodes and intelligent coordinators matter beyond simple unit counts. |
| Z-M03 | pp. 53–54: early infestation uses spores, soil contamination and establishment of hive growth before overt assault on colonies. | Zerg encounters can be investigation, environmental horror, containment and survival—not just combat. |
| Z-M04 | p. 55: Zerg are different absorbed breeds, produced via Larvae; Creep is a living supporting substrate for structures; organisms exhibit unusual regeneration. | Create organism profiles, Creep/infestation hazards and biological structures, not manufacturing mechanics. |
| Z-M05 | pp. 56–57: Overlords are coordination, sensory and some transport platforms; Zerglings are lightly intelligent assault/scout animals effective in groups. | A swarm's tactical capability depends partly on command structure; sensory/detection abilities can be separate. |
| Z-M06 | pp. 68–69: the brood listings describe differing functions and intelligence estimates (Tiamat command, Fenris pursuit, Baelrog terror, etc.) from **Terran scientists/scouts**. | Broods offer operational doctrines and scenario variety. Estimates and observers' uncertainty must be tagged, not treated as omniscient fact. |

**Inference:** Consider separate GM behaviors for *coordinated*, *isolated/feral* and *elite-controlled* swarm encounters, starting with SWADE tactics/Leadership/Quick Encounters before adding a universal hive rule. Later Blizzard Q&A provides independent discussion of feral behavior; see `lore/entries/zerg/feral-zerg.md`.

**Temporal caution:** The manual describes the Overmind's Swarm circa the original game. It does not establish how every Zerg hierarchy works under Kerrigan, later broodmothers, or primal Zerg.

## C. Protoss findings: history, cultural schisms and psionic technology

| Claim | Direct manual evidence | RPG importance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P-M01 | pp. 71–73: ancient xel'naga history is introduced as fragmentary Protoss texts/traditions; later events include the Aeon of Strife. | Separate *Protoss belief* from later canonical revelation. Do not silently overwrite ancient history. |
| P-M02 | pp. 74–75: Khas rediscovers the communal psionic link and formulates the Khala; society becomes Judicator, Khalai and Templar castes. | Protoss PCs need cultural and ideological identity independent of a “Zealot class.” |
| P-M03 | p. 75: the Dae'Uhl is a non-interference-oriented stewardship ideal, despite Protoss military and technological superiority. | Enables diplomatic, reconnaissance, investigative and morally complicated Protoss campaigns. |
| P-M04 | pp. 75–77: Adun and the Dark Templar schism involve opposition to the Conclave and different use of psionic power. | Major cultural difference, but not a second biological species automatically. |
| P-M05 | p. 79: defensive psionic shields are engineered into field generators, protecting even small machines; fields can be depleted and regenerate, with Shield Batteries accelerating recovery. | **Required shield behavior** for eventual rules tests; do not automatically copy RTS shield points. |
| P-M06 | pp. 79–80: Probes position warp anchors for prefabricated structures; the Psi Matrix powers important installations. | Useful in stories featuring infrastructure sabotage/relocation; avoid equating the RTS on-screen power radius to a universal real-world measurement. |
| P-M07 | p. 80: Zealots use powered suits, cybernetic enhancements and psionic blades; Dragoons contain severely wounded volunteer warriors. | Equipment/cyberware/psionics can be separate, while Dragoons require walker and life-support design. |
| P-M08 | pp. 88–90: historical tribes retain distinct political roles and traditions despite caste organization. | Distinguish tribe, caste, ideology and occupation; no single protoss personality or PC class. |

**Inference:** Protoss PCs should have a strong default psionic background but not all individuals need identical powers. Technological shields, psionic blade manifestation, inherent telepathy and cultural restrictions should not be conflated. Mixed Terran–Protoss party balance is a campaign-design question, not a justification for weakening all Protoss.

## D. First continuity comparison: original xel'naga account vs later narrative
- **Manual pp. 51–53 and 71–73:** A broad experimenter-centered narrative, with the Protoss section explicitly rooted in ancient fragmentary records and traditions.
- **Blizzard Story Primer, Part 1:** Introduces the Infinite Cycle and assigns crucial interference to Amon, who clashes with other xel'naga: https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23331587/starcraft-story-primer .
- **Disposition:** **Later expansion/reframing; exact contradictions require claim-level evaluation.** The broad original account should not be presented as a timeless omniscient narration that rules out later events, but neither should all early history be summarily marked false. Updated discussion: `lore/disputes/xelnaga-history.md`.

## E. What this manual does *not* establish
- The exact real-life height/mass/volume of every unit, even where illustrations accompany entries.
- A universal damage or penetration formula; descriptions such as rifle armor penetration require realistic SWADE calibration rather than literal armor immunity bypass.
- That only the gear shown in an RTS unit profile is available to that military profession.
- That every later Dominion soldier has the same resocialization history as a typical Confederate Marine in 1998 source material.
- That cultural ideology, military role and biological ancestry are interchangeable.
- That Zerg production time, Protoss shield values, or detection radii can be converted 1:1 from game UI.
