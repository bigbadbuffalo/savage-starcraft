# Xel'naga — early historical account and later Amon revelations

**Status:** **Later expansion and partial reinterpretation observed; specific direct contradictions not yet adjudicated.**  
**Research state:** First-pass comparison of the 1998 manual and Blizzard Story Primer completed.  
**Logged:** 2026-10-10; updated in Phase 2.

## Early material now examined
**SC98-MANUAL (P1):**
- Printed pp. **51–53**, “The Grand Experiment,” “The Birth of the Zerg,” “Overmind” and “The Fall of the Xel'Naga”: describes a collective program of genetic experimentation, failed/abandoned Protoss experiment, Zerg on Zerus, Overmind and destruction of xel'naga ships.
- Printed pp. **71–73**, “The Xel'Naga and the First Born”: explicitly begins with **fragmentary ancient Protoss texts and traditions**. This is a valuable signal that at least some deep-time narrative is presented from an in-universe historical perspective, not as absolutely complete knowledge.

Facsimile consulted page by page: https://manuals.plus/m/fdb4dcccc69a2f7c1d7d8c67e0930bc42a3687005dd62270539cb64f3a3fabb0.pdf .

## Later published summary
**SRC-BLIZZARD-STORY-PRIMER (P3)**, “Part 1: The Distant Past”:
https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23331587/starcraft-story-primer

- Describes an Infinite Cycle in which two species reach purity of form and essence and ascend.
- Identifies **Amon** as a rebellious xel'naga who directly manipulated protoss and zerg.
- Distinguishes Amon from other xel'naga and describes their response and Amon's defeat.

## Narrow disposition
The later source adds intentionality, individual actors and xel'naga internal conflict to the early account. Those may **qualify or reframe** broad collective references to “the Xel'naga” in the old manual. The older Protoss text's explicit fragmentary/traditional framing helps explain how knowledge might be incomplete, but it does **not automatically explain every discrepancy** in the narrator or grant permission to invent a retcon.

We **must not** (a) suppress the original account, (b) treat all early xel'naga behavior as certainly Amon's without source tracing, or (c) assert the two sources are wholly irreconcilable on the basis of broad summaries.

## Research still needed
Compare precise events from the original and StarCraft II campaigns (especially Zerus and Legacy of the Void revelations), identify individual contradictory propositions (agent, location, causal sequence, outcomes), and enter separate claim records for any actual mismatch.

## RPG impact
Use the perspective appropriate to the campaign's era. Mystery about xel'naga is an intentional historical/worldview feature. Do not encode uncertain ancient origin claims into ancestry rules or technology before they matter to play.
