# C-14 “Impaler” Gauss Rifle — Lore Evidence

**Research state:** collecting; direct P1 1998 manual confirmed, later works listed below are **indirect evidence pending firsthand inspection**.  
**Scope:** Main Confederate-era C-14 and later Dominion derivatives; avoid silently blending C-14 and AGR-14, C-141 AC, or every gauss rifle.  
**Last checked:** 2026-10-10.

## 1. Primary claims directly supported by the 1998 manual

**Source:** SC98-MANUAL, printed p. **35**, Terran Units — **Marine**. Official Blizzard original: https://ftp.blizzard.com/pub/misc/StarCraft.PDF ; original-manual facsimile inspected in `lore/research/SC98-MANUAL-AUDIT.md`.

| Claim ID | Proposition | Evidence type | Authority | Confidence / qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C14-P1-01 | The C-14 “Impaler” Gauss Rifle is standard equipment in the original-era Confederate Marine's profile. | Explicit equipment listing, p. 35 | P1 | High in that period; does not require *every* Marine to carry one |
| C14-P1-02 | It launches **8 mm metal spikes** at **hypersonic speeds**. | Explicit descriptive text, p. 35 | P1 | High for caliber and qualitative velocity; projectile length, mass and exact velocity **not provided** |
| C14-P1-03 | Its rounds are designed for maximum armor penetration. | Explicit descriptive text, p. 35 | P1 | High for design intention; not a numeric value of penetration or universal immunity bypass |
| C14-P1-04 | A capacitor system enforces short controlled bursts to conserve ammunition and power. | Explicit description, p. 35 | P1 | High; no exact burst size/cadence supplied |
| C14-P1-05 | The same infantry profile lists a **CMC-300/400 powered combat suit** with life support and NBC protection. | Explicit equipment listing/description, p. 35 | P1 | High for standard combination; does not by itself prove that an unarmored person can never operate the rifle |

**Important:** A big weapon in an illustration is visual evidence of a bulky design, not a canon technical length/weight measurement. The term "rifle" is source nomenclature, not a rule that it must share modern 5.56 mm rifle stats.

## 3. Interpreting the weapon's military role
- **Strong inference:** This is a high-powered, sustained/burst automatic infantry weapon optimized for power-armored forces, with armor-penetrating spikes. The rifle's label doesn't constrain its SWADE weapon classification.
- **Not established:** It is universally crew-served or always unusable by unarmored humans.
- **Not established:** Every Marine's variant has one specific round count, barrel size, mass, or alternate ammunition design.
- **Not established:** Its 8mm projectiles have any one uniquely calculable kinetic energy. Cross-section does not supply spike mass, length or exact muzzle velocity.
- **Not established:** “Maximum penetration” makes it an antitank gun or able to sink warships. Heavy Weapon/Armor interactions must be tested mechanically.
- **Distinguish technology:** A magnetic *assist* device or coilgun mechanism does not necessarily entail no chemical propellant; later Field Manual material reportedly uses both. Flag that P3 detail for direct source verification rather than reconstructing physics from the name.

## 4. RPG handoff
The **natural mechanical home** is a detachable firearm, with potential compatible CMC stabilizer/power assistance and separate ammunition or attachments as needed. Do **not** create a Marine character class or automatically bolt a C-14 into every power suit.

The first proposed numerical test is deliberately located in `development/benchmarks/C14-MECHANICAL-STUDY.md`, distinct from lore. It may earn `Heavy Weapon` in SWADE even though it is *issued to infantry*: classification is an armor-damage interaction, not the number of people carrying the gun.
