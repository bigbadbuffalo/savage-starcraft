**Design implications (not canon statements):** Separate inherent psionic talent, trained powers, caste/tribal culture and issued hardware. Devise shielding with SWADE protection and recharge rules only after reviewing existing mechanical precedents. Do not apply the original Khala political structure unchanged to every later era (see SC2-LOTV).

## Additional Blizzard-source audit — armored suits and caste psi (2026-10-11)
The official original [Zealot unit lore](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/units/zealot.shtml) explicitly calls Zealot equipment a **power suit that enhances and channels innate psionics**, forming a defensive personal shield and projecting forearm energy blades. A later [developer retrospective](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/21509420/evolution-complete-reimagining-classic-starcraft-units-for-starcraft-ii) independently describes Zealots wearing **ornate power armor**. It does **not** establish the Terran CMC **mechanical armor class, Mods and Strength** structure applies identically.

Original [plasma shield strategy account](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/bstrat.shtml) and later [SC2 shield guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/5963101/game-guide-protoss-shields) say shields **absorb physical/energy harm ahead of hull/body protection**, deplete and recharge and also equip machines; shields are **technological/psionic field emitters**, not purely the personal's Vigor. Special Sentry placed Force Fields are **different battlefield-wall tech**. [Creative Q&A 13](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/8501031/starcraft-ii-creative-development-q-a-part-13) identifies separate Protoss castes and trained specialties; [Selendis's post-Khala story](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23485205/short-story-one-people-one-purpose) distinguishes loss of interlinked **Khala gestalt** from the ongoing individuality of Protoss.
