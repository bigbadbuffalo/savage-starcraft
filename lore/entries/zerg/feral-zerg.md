## RPG implications (not game mechanics)
- A Zerg organism does not cease to function simply because it lacks an immediate controller.
- Tactical coordination is a major distinction between guided swarms and uncontrolled predators.
- A feral encounter might feel different from a directed swarm without requiring an entirely new ancestry or stat block.
