# Hydralisk — Ranged Assault Organism

**Status:** `playtest`, proposed 2026-10-11.  
**Blizzard:** [1998 Hydralisk guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/zerg/units/hydralisk.shtml) describes a powerful plated organism firing **many high-speed needle spines, penetrating 2 cm neosteel**, capable against ground/air and relatively slow on open terrain. This supports actual anti-armor **penetration ability**, **not** a known SWADE AP value or range.  
**Body reference:** Fantasy **Giant Scorpion**, printed **pp.207–208**: **Size3**, d12+1 Strength, d10 Vigor, Armor+4, Toughness14(4); Sci-Fi **Decapitator**, **p.241**: forelimb scythes Str+d10 AP4, natural armored predator.

## Playtest stat line
**Hydralisk (Extra, Size 3, Normal Scale)**  
**Attributes:** Agility **d8**, Smarts **d4 (A)**, Spirit **d8**, Strength **d12+1**, Vigor **d10**.  
**Skills:** Athletics **d6**, Fighting **d8**, Intimidation **d6**, Notice **d8**, Shooting **d8**, Stealth **d6**.  
**Pace:** **6**; **Parry:** **6**; **Toughness:** **14 (4)**. **Edges:** —.

**Special Abilities**
- **Armor +4:** Hardened upper carapace, **ordinary natural Armor**, not automatically Heavy Armor.
- **Needle Spines:** **Range 18/36/72**, **Damage 2d8**, **AP4**, **RoF1**, **Heavy Weapon, Class I** (average 9+AP4=13 = I) — provisional conversion from official neosteel-piercing description. Spines are organically grown; do not use ordinary industrial 30-shot magazines, but rate of supply/biological recovery isn't an unlimited mechanical bonus.
- **Talons and Fangs:** **Str+d6**, **AP2** (normal melee weapon, not Heavy Weapon).
- **Broad Targeting:** Spine range can engage aerial targets if seen and within normal shooting/Scale/visibility constraints; **no free anti-air fire-control modifier**.
- **Option — Burrowed Hydralisk:** Some strains add Core **Burrow (2)** when appropriately evolved. It cannot use the Lurker's underground spine-line attack simply because both species burrow.

**Designer comparison:** Against a CMC Marine **Class I**, its Class I spine can deliver **1d3 Heavy Metal Wounds on hit**, +1 on Shooting raise, before Soak. Against an unarmored colonist or ordinary Zerg body it rolls **2d8/AP4 normally**; not a mass of automatic hits because hundreds of biological spines exist. Against our +14/ClassII Firebat it normally cannot inflict direct Class Wounds without a raise. These are dangerous but clear roles.

**Mechanical conflict to test:** Size3 and Armor+4 make the Hydralisk physically robust, but **CMC C-14's AP4 negates all that natural Armor** and its damage rolls normally; don't mistakenly invoke a Heavy Metal Class just because the spine attack itself is HW. If the Hydralisk is too tough/fragile, tune Vigor/Armor with all-faction ecology.
