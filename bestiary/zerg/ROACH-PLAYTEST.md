# Roach — Burrowing Acid Skirmisher

**Status:** `playtest`, proposed 2026-10-11.  
**Official role:** Blizzard's [StarCraft II Zerg tactics](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/5838590/game-guide-zerg-attacks) explicitly depicts Roaches **attacking, burrowing, regenerating and emerging again**, potentially tunneling underground; the [basic Zerg guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/5740264/game-guide-zerg-basics) explains the species' biological recovery theme. These are primary gameplay descriptions, not a quantified biological healing rate.  
**Shape baseline:** Fantasy **Giant Scorpion** (pp.207–208) chitin/legs, scaled down to **Size2**; Sci-Fi **Death Worm** (pp.240–241) burrowing and ranged bodily projectile; **Core Regeneration** p.177 / **Resilient** p.178.

## Playtest stat line
**Roach (Resilient Extra, Size 2, Normal Scale)**  
**Attributes:** Agility **d6**, Smarts **d4 (A)**, Spirit **d8**, Strength **d12**, Vigor **d10**.  
**Skills:** Athletics **d6**, Fighting **d6**, Notice **d8**, Shooting **d8**, Stealth **d8**.  
**Pace:** **6**; **Parry:** **5**; **Toughness:** **13 (4)**. **Edges:** —.

**Special Abilities**
- **Armor +4:** Heavy-looking organic chitin, **ordinary Armor**, *not automatically classed Heavy Armor*.
- **Acid Spit:** **Range 12/24/48**, **Damage 2d8**, **AP4**, **RoF1**, **Heavy Weapon, Class I** *provisional* (avg9+4=13 Class I). This is a narrow **project assumption** for a penetrating organic acid strike: the Blizzard tactical guide does not confirm an AP4 or a Heavy Weapon category. It is single-target unless a distinct bile-spread strain is designed; **no unpriced Damage-over-Time/Hardness loss** on every hit.
- **Bite/Claws:** **Str+d4**, normal damage.
- **Burrow (4):** Tunnels under soft soil at Pace4; surprise and detection use Core pp.175–176. Must emerge to spit acid in this entry; not automatically able to attack fully submerged as a Lurker does.
- **Resilient:** As an **Extra** it can take **one Wound** before being Incapacitated (Core p.178).
- **Burrowed Fast Regeneration (conditional):** While completely underground, it may attempt **one Vigor roll per round**, healing one Wound on success and another on raise under Core p.177. **This is a highly consequential optional/flagged playtest choice**, not a fully verified lore biological rate. It is unable to attack above ground in the same state; a GM can choose normal post-combat healing if this overwhelms combat pacing. No regeneration of corrosively destroyed/massively obliterated tissue without separate rationale.

**Counterplay:** Scanner/motion detector, striking it before it burrows, forcing it above ground, incendiary area attacks and encirclement; don't turn Burrow into impervious underground invulnerability. Natural Armor is bypassed by appropriate AP. Resilient Wounds apply before it enters ordinary incapacitation (not a second unrelated pool).

**Validation priorities:** Benchmark +4 Armor combined with Resilient + per-round Vigor healing; decide whether **Fast** healing needs a delay or Slow option only after integrated combat.
