# Zergling — Pack Assault Organism

**Status:** `playtest`, proposed 2026-10-11. **Blizzard:** [original unit description](https://classic.battle.net/scc/zerg/units/zergling.shtml) documents canine/runner ancestry, fast movement, blades and fangs, pack lethality, and optional evolved Burrow / Metabolic Boost / Adrenal Glands. **SWADE baseline:** Core **Dire Wolf**, printed **p.181**, physical predator Pace10, Str d8, Vig d8, bite Str+d6; Fantasy **Velociraptor** printed **p.188** (Agility, armor, predatory tactics). Special add-on **Fantasy Gnoll Pack Tactics** printed **p.209** (Gang Up added to Fighting damage), with SFC Throxian Soldier **p.289** as coordinated-swarm behavioral comparison.

## Playtest stat line
**Zergling (Extra, Size 0, Normal Scale)**  
**Attributes:** Agility **d8**, Smarts **d4 (A)**, Spirit **d6**, Strength **d8**, Vigor **d8**.  
**Skills:** Athletics **d8**, Fighting **d8**, Intimidation **d6**, Notice **d6**, Stealth **d8**, Survival **d6**.  
**Pace:** **10**; **Parry:** **6**; **Toughness:** **8 (2)**; **Running:** d10. **Edges:** —.

**Special Abilities**
- **Armor +2:** Hard natural exoskeleton. **Ordinary Armor; not Heavy Armor**, and no Heavy Metal Class.
- **Ripping Claws and Jaws:** **Str+d6** melee damage, ordinary natural attack.
- **Pack Tactics:** The Zergling adds its **normal Gang Up bonus to Fighting damage** against a target (Fantasy Gnoll precedent). Gang Up still follows its usual cap/requirements and the bonus is **not** gained when alone.
- **Relentless Pursuit:** Pace 10 and d10 Running die from Dire Wolf speed.
- **Animal/Swarm Control:** Smarts d4 (A) is a game-building abstraction, not a claim that it lacks telepathic direction. Brood commanders can convey tactical orders through ordinary scene-specific control; **no automatic unlimited-range telepathy modifier** is conferred.

**Variants (not standard issue)**
- **Burrowing strain:** Core **Burrow (3)**, requiring suitable soil, concealed emergence and normal Notice/Stealth opposed detection (Core pp.175–176). **Not** every Zergling can burrow in every era without the evolution.
- **Metabolic evolution:** Increase Pace to **12** and Running die to **d12** (original provisional tabletop rate, *not* copied RTS percentage).
- **Adrenal strain:** **Frenzy** (or Improved Frenzy for named boss breed only), *not* automatic free extra actions for the whole swarm.
- **Hunter-killer pounce:** optionally use SFC Throxian Soldier **Pounce** (+4 rather than +2 damage on Wild Attack), but avoid stacking this on every ordinary Zergling with Pack Tactics before testing.

**Rationale and warnings:** The Core wolf handles body mass/speed, the Fantasy predator/scaled chitin adds natural defense, and Pack Tactics supplies a sharp reason several Zerglings threaten a Marine. The weapon is *not* HW: a single ordinary Zergling typically **cannot penetrate sealed Class I Heavy Armor** merely with claw damage, absent vulnerability/equipment breaches/GM situational effects. This is a **known testable tension** with fiction where swarms can overwhelm armored soldiers. Don't silently grant every claw a Class II attack. Test grapples, called shots, mass knockdowns and armor breach rules in the linked encounter instead.

**Integrated check:** Four to eight Zergling Extras versus one CMC Marine, plus an unarmored colonist; C-14 rolled damage resolves against normal Armor +2. Normal Gang Up applies, and Pack Tactics adds the same Gang Up to *damage*, not an independent stacking pile of +2s.
