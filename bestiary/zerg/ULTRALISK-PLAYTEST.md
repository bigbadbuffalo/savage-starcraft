# Ultralisk — Exceptional Biological Heavy Assault

**Status:** `playtest`, **corrected vehicle-calibrated Class III biological creature** as of 2026-10-11 after direct Vehicle Guide p.21 and SFC Heavy Metal p.93 review; no final owner approval. The historical T24(8)/claimed ClassIII prototype was inconsistent (ClassII by RAW Toughness), superseded by T26(10). See `../../development/benchmarks/BIOLOGICAL-VEHICLE-SCALE-STUDY.md`.  
**Primary source:** Blizzard's [original Ultralisk guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/zerg/units/ultralisk.shtml): living tank, enormous, **monomolecular-edge Kaiser blades** able to cut armored vehicles and soldiers. A Blizzard-hosted [War Stories anthology preview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/14905498/war-stories-ebook-now-available) presents a narrator whose repeated C-14 fire barely affects the enormous organism; this is **character experience in narrative**, not a universal armor measurement.  
**Anatomical references:** Fantasy **Tyrannosaurus Rex** printed **p.188**, Size7 predator, 16(3) Toughness, bite/AP4, Resilient; **War Elephant p.196** for body mass, charge and Sweep (Imp); SFC **Deep Space Worm p.242** and **Throxian Bombard p.288** expressly show **Heavy Armor living organisms**. Chosen **Size 8** sits in Core printed p.179 **Huge** and is a **first species-scale hypothesis**, not a certified Ultralisk dimension. Different game-era depictions may require Size7, 8, 10 or 12 variants.

## Playtest stat line
**Ultralisk (Size 8, Huge; usually a major Extra / named Wild Card)**  
**Attributes:** Agility **d6**, Smarts **d4 (A)**, Spirit **d10**, Strength **d12+8**, Vigor **d12**.  
**Skills:** Athletics **d8**, Fighting **d10**, Intimidation **d12**, Notice **d8**, Stealth **d4**.  
**Pace:** **8**; **Parry:** **7**; **Toughness:** **26 (10 Heavy Armor)**. **Edges:** **Improved Sweep**, **Nerves of Steel** (War Elephant precedents, subject to actual published eligibility), otherwise —.

**Special Abilities**
- **Exceptional Chitinous Heavy Armor +10; derived Class III (provisional):** Ordinary Zerg shells are not automatically Heavy Armor. **Toughness26 = Vigor d12 base8 + Size8 + Armor10**; by SFC p.93 total Toughness26 derives **ClassIII**, so no automatic extension of the project's *power-suit-only fixed Class* policy to organisms. ClassI C-14 has **No Effect** even on attack raise; ClassII anti-materiel weapon inflicts 0 Wounds normally/1 on raise; ClassIII rocket causes 1d3 on a qualifying hit before creature Soak. SFC AP warhead reduces target Class by one at **Armor10+**, so K12 AP now reduces III→II, allowing ClassI K12 **0 Wounds ordinarily, 1 on raise**. This +10 armor is an RPG hypothesis, not canonical shell thickness.
- **Kaiser Blades:** **Str+d10, AP12, Heavy Weapon, Weapon Class III**, Reach **2**. Calculation for Str **d12+8** average **14.5** + average d10 **5.5** + AP12 = **32** (Heavy Metal Class III). This is an original mechanical conversion of "monomolecular cutting armored vehicle"; **not** a canon amount of neosteel penetrated. Use ordinary Fighting and multiple-target rules; **no free vehicle critical chart or auto-bisect**.
- **Massive Breakthrough:** Its **Huge Size8** naturally grants **+2 Wounds, Reach 2, Scale modifier +4**, as printed Core p.179; do not also buy an Extra/vehicle independent Wound track or add Resilient without another reason.
- **Improved Sweep:** Uses the printed SWADE Edge to attack several adjacent enemies, respecting conditions and action costs. A single blade swing may menace multiple Marines, **not unlimited hordes for free**.
- **Fear (initial encounter only):** Foes unused to enormous Zerg monstrosities make the appropriate **Core Fear check** when suddenly confronting one; may be omitted if the campaign regularly deploys Ultralisks. Fear is situational, not an automatic repeated morale tax.
- **Stomp/Crush:** Against creatures directly underfoot during a charge or grapple, use **Str+d8 Heavy Weapon Class II** as a close-area/GM controlled option only after checking movement and target positioning. **Not** an automatic second attack every turn; it should not eclipse Kaiser Blades. Consider Giant Foes interaction when appropriate.

**Encounter roles:** A handful of C-14 Marines *without* Class III support should prioritize escape, terrain, pursuit avoidance, medical evacuation and calling artillery rather than rolling rifle attacks until it dies. A Marine squad equipped with an SFC **Class III rocket** has a credible tool; compare an Armored Class III tank target and vehicle hazards separately.

**Important caveat:** Even with corrected Toughness26 and RAW-derived ClassIII, a Huge-size Extra can take the two additional Wounds from Size before Incapacitation; a Huge Wild Card's exact Wound capacity follows the printed Size rules. No special immunity to every environmental hazard, psionics or toxins is inferred.

## Monster as Vehicle comparison (2026-10-11)
The *Vehicle Guide* **p.21 "Monsters as Vehicles"** specifically demonstrates designing a **creature** from vehicle Toughness/Size comparisons, returning to the **Core Size Table for Strength and special abilities**, not automatically giving it a mechanical Crew, Handling, Out-of-Control or vehicle Wound track. Our **ordinary Ultralisk remains a Size8 Huge creature** with standard Fighting/Soak, biological Wounds and +2 Size Wounds, but vehicle-calibrated **T26(10) / ClassIII**. **Alternative legendary siege boss (unapproved):** SFC *Medium Brawler walker* **p.188 Size8, d12+8, T30(10), ClassIII, five vehicle Wounds**. That would require **one vehicle-style Wound track and adapted biological Critical Hits**, never stacked with Huge creature Wounds.
