# Zerg Creep, Detection, Brood Signals and Infestation — Prototype Hazards

**Status:** `playtest` scene/hazard toolkit; not one universal 2026-10-11 infestation mechanic.  
**Lore:** Blizzard original Zerg units and hive organization, SC2 [Zerg basics](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/5740264/game-guide-zerg-basics) (regeneration and burrow) and [Roach tactics](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/5838590/game-guide-zerg-attacks). Co-op/Heroes of the Storm's [Zagara commentary](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/14569140/developer-insights-spillin-the-creep-on-zagara) has creep providing speed, sight, and regeneration **in those game systems**; those are inspiration, **not automatic universal RPG buffs**.

## A. Physical creep substrate — normal scene rules
**Creep-Matted Terrain:** A room/path filled with thick living fibrous mats counts as **Difficult Ground for unfamiliar non-Zerg characters** if it tangibly obstructs boots/wheels; shallow smooth creep doesn't automatically impede everyone. **Normal Core terrain rules** apply; a Marine's CMC suit gives environmental sealing and strength but **does not** confer immunity to getting tangled or bogged down.
**Creep-Covered Passage:** As a GM scene hazard, use ordinary **Notice/Survival** to spot biologically active traps and **Repair/Science** to identify mechanical weak points/biochemical lifelines; damaged walls, biofilm/cysts and roots change movement, concealment and exit options rather than imposing a universal -2 trait modifier. Nydus/infested passages await future statting.

## B. Burrowed sensing and alarms
**Buried watchers:** SFC *Lesser Xenos* printed **p.236** **Croakers** (vibration alarms) and **Alarm Birds** (predator-luring) are excellent *noncombat logic* analogues for small detecting parasites/creep nodes. On sufficient vibrations/noise, the node calls a nearby patrol in **1d4 rounds** as an original GM encounter timer (not a Blizzard biological rate). Detect with Notice/Science, disable with Repair, Athletics, or stealth/quiet passage; avoid requiring a single mandatory skill.
**Burrowed Zerg:** Use Core pp.175–176 opposed **Stealth/Notice**; SFC *Death Worm* pp.240–241 demonstrates firing from beneath Near Total Cover. **Ground sensors should reveal location** but may not negate cover or make soil transparent.

## C. Infection vs a sealed suit
**Infestation spores / burrowing parasites:** Horror **Corpse Worm Swarm** pp.112–113 and SFC **Bore Worm** p.287 show skin-contact parasitism and ongoing injury. Core/Horror Swarm **p.162 Horror** specifically protects fully sealed suits against tiny parasite attacks. **Use these mechanics only for appropriate spore/parasite-rich infested areas**, not every patch of creep.
**Example hazard:** Entering a punctured ventilation chamber containing active spores, a person **without a functional full seal** must make **Vigor at −2 once upon exposure** or become **Fatigued**. Cure via extraction, cleansing and standard disease/Healing handling; additional disease progression is scenario-specific. This is an **original suggested hazard check**, not an independently observed biological Zerg spore toxin rule. Worn functioning CMC with base Sealed property defeats ordinary inhalation; damaged seals are a meaningful risk.
**Alternate fiction:** SFC *Stygian Midge* **p.236** makes sensor-obscuring Pitch Darkness for IR but not sonar; optional spore cloud could block optics yet leave seismic/sonar available. Don't stack total sensory denial for free.

## D. Brood coordination and behavior
**Normal swarm with leader:** Start with SWADE **Gang Up**, ordinary positioning and coordinated attacks. Throxian **Soldier Hive Mind** (SFC **p.289**) and **Enceph Hive Mind Focus** (SFC **p.288**) offer a native model of local psychic command. An Overlord/Queen/Cerebrate could allow quiet coordination, not flat blanket combat bonuses.
**Wild/Feral Zerg:** If brood control is disrupted, Zerg do not necessarily die or forget how to move. Use altered tactics, tests and environmental observations based on `../../lore/entries/zerg/feral-zerg.md` and `../../lore/entries/zerg/swarm-control.md`; **don't invent a universal immediate -2 to all rolls upon losing an Overlord**.
**Swarm-scale mass:** 20+ individual Zerglings should use **Quick Encounters or Mass Battle/Clash tools** when drama warrants rather than tracking a Core Swarm as if all creatures were mites. Core Swarm piercing immunity and sealed-suit immunity are wrong for normal dog-sized attackers.

## E. Short encounter prompts
1. **Silent ducts:** Creep Alarm Node sends Zerglings unless discovered and disabled; normal closed CMC armor prevents spore inhalation while exposed civilian suffers a hazard roll.
2. **Buried ambusher:** One Lurker behind difficult ground, sensors permit discovery and positioning, Civvie construction explosives could remove ground concealment.
3. **Roach hit-and-run:** Two burrowing Roaches attack then disengage to heal, testing conditional Regeneration against scanner-supported C-14 Marines.
4. **Evacuation under breakthrough:** Ultralisk threatens vehicle loading zone: artillery/rocket support and Dramatic Task rather than routine rifle volley.

**No canon promotion:** Spore-hazard Vigor modifier, patrol delay and physical difficult-ground assumptions are **scenario tools** chosen for gameplay; a Protoss world or strain variant may have different creep structure/biology.
