# Lurker — Subterranean Spike Ambusher

**Status:** `playtest` conversion, 2026-10-11; baseline species **not** the same as SFC "Ravager".  
**Blizzard:** [Original Lurker Guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/zerg/units/lurker.shtml) and [Brood War FAQ](https://classic.battle.net/scc/faq/bw.shtml): Hydralisk metamorphosis, natural subterranean spines in a **straight line** through multiple ground targets, **burrow-only attack**, largely defenseless while moving on surface. The attack can affect friendlies; it does not attack aircraft.  
**Physical starting point:** SFC **Death Worm** printed pp.240–241 (Burrow, attack from below with near-total cover) + Fantasy **Giant Scorpion** pp.207–208 (chitin and limb morphology), scaled to **Size4 Large**. This is not an exact certified canonical body size.

## Playtest stat line
**Lurker (Extra, Size 4 Large, +1 Size Wound, Reach 1)**  
**Attributes:** Agility **d6**, Smarts **d4 (A)**, Spirit **d8**, Strength **d12+2**, Vigor **d12**.  
**Skills:** Athletics **d8**, Fighting **d6**, Notice **d8**, Shooting **d8**, Stealth **d10**.  
**Pace:** **6** on surface; **Parry:** **5**; **Toughness:** **16 (4)**. **Edges:** —.

**Special Abilities**
- **Armor +4:** Heavy-looking but **ordinary natural Armor**, not a creature Heavy Metal Class.
- **Burrow (3):** Can sink/move through loose earth as a Core creature burrower. While underground may use its surface **Stealth d10 versus Notice** and relevant sensor checks. Against a detected/revealed organism, attack coverage/area rules apply; **not invincible just because below ground**.
- **Subterranean Spine Eruption:** **Can fire ONLY while burrowed and stationary**. From its location choose a **12-inch straight, 1-inch wide line** (corresponding to a **24-yard long lane** using SWADE table scale); make **Shooting d8** as a natural area attack, resolving against *everyone in the line* with normal area-attack and Evasion procedures when aware. **Damage 3d10, AP4, Heavy Weapon Class II** (average 16.5+4 = 20.5, rounds up to **21**, Class II). The line template width is an explicit **Savage StarCraft homebrew geometry**, NOT an existing printed SFC template nor an RTS range measurement. It cannot target flying creatures above the ground, and it can injure friendlies in the path as the original guide indicates.
- **Exposure limitation:** While above ground, Lurker has only a weak **Str+d4** biting attack, cannot unleash spine eruption. Surface movement/reburrowing costs appropriate normal actions; no free buried missile attack plus full underground relocation in the same action.
- **Large:** Size4 grants one extra Wound and Reach+1 by the Core p.179 Size Table. **Do not also add Resilient merely because it looks armored**.

**Counters and balance:** Motion scanners/seismic detectors, approaching outside the firing line, flying units, grenades/area effects, weapons reaching its revealed burrow position, forcing it to surface, flanking. A Lurker's buried **cover** can be extremely protective: the SFC Death Worm precedent fires from Near Total Cover −8; **do not treat detection alone as stripping all physical cover**. A scanner can locate it but a penetrating weapon or terrain alteration may still be required.

**Class interaction:** Versus **Class I CMC** spine ClassII deals **1d3+1** direct Wounds before Soak; against **Class II heavy suit** same ClassII gives **1d3**; against **natural non-Heavy Armor** the rolled 3d10/AP4 attacks apply, and each qualifying target is independently resolved. The creature's ***attack*** being Class II does *not* magically turn its own hide into Heavy Armor.

**Open:** line length/width, buried cover/injury counterplay, stationarity, and blast interactions need a tabletop dry run. Don't map game tile sizes into yards uncritically.
