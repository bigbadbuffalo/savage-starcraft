# Baneling — Acidic Suicide Assault Organism

**Status:** `playtest`, proposed 2026-10-11.  
**Role:** Volatile short-lived Zerg-derived assault organism detonating acidic fluids in a radius. The RTS/co-op/game depictions support its self-destructive attack; details such as rupture-on-death, persistent acid or bonus building damage vary and are not automatically canon. Blizzard [Zagara developer commentary](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/14569140/developer-insights-spillin-the-creep-on-zagara) references Banelings exploding for area damage (a crossover mechanics description, not numeric lore data).  
**Physical ancestor:** Core **Dire Wolf/Zergling Size0** morphology with distended abdomen; mechanical surprising match: **SFC Lesser Xenos 'Firefly' p.236** causes its own **large explosive fire blast**, showing organic area detonation; **Super Powers Companion Explode p.62** can inform explosion area but **allows the exploding character to survive/reassemble and must NOT be transplanted unchanged**.

## Playtest stat line
**Baneling (Extra, Size 0, Normal Scale)**  
**Attributes:** Agility **d8**, Smarts **d4 (A)**, Spirit **d6**, Strength **d8**, Vigor **d8**.  
**Skills:** Athletics **d8**, Fighting **d4**, Notice **d6**, Stealth **d6**.  
**Pace:** **8**; **Parry:** **4**; **Toughness:** **7 (1)**. **Edges:** —.

**Special Abilities**
- **Armor +1:** Hardened cyst plating, **ordinary Armor**.
- **Unstable Acid Cysts (Detonate):** **One action**, **self-destructive**, centered at the Baneling's occupied location, **Medium Blast Template**, **4d8/AP4**, **Heavy Weapon, Class II** (average 18+4=22; 21–30 Class II); use the Core blast/Evasion procedure for creatures aware of the attack, and resolve ordinary rolled corrosive damage against unclassed targets. When it detonates, **remove/incapacitate the Baneling immediately**; it does *not* reform like an SPC super with Explode.
- **Corrosive Trapping:** Bioacid, not environmental **fire**. CMC-660 Fire-Resistant Insulation's −4 against **fire/heat** does **not** apply to a Baneling acid detonation. Acid might damage objects by normal rules; no automatic stacking permanent Armor degradation.
- **Uncontrolled rupture on death:** **Not default**, because whether all killed Banelings detonate exactly like deliberate self-sacrifice needs the right source/variant. A GM may add a distinct *unstable death-burst variant* after checking exposure and warning/area fairness.

**Heavy Metal encounter role:** Its deliberately ClassII payload threatens Class I Marine (**1d3+1 direct Wounds**) and Class II Firebat/Marauder (**1d3**, before Soak), but falls well short of guaranteed tank destruction at Class III. Its one-use nature balances unusually dangerous attack dice; **this is a hypothesis to validate against actual swarm deployment numbers**, not a direct RTS suicide-damage conversion. The armor is NOT Heavy and does not shield the organism from C-14.

**Important design risk:** Multiple Banelings with overlapping MBTs can overwhelm Extra PCs/Marine squads too easily if the GM ignores Evasion, detection, positioning and the one-use action cost. Test 1–3 attackers separately before doubling spawns.
