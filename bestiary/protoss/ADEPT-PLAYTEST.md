# Adept — Mobile Ranged Protoss Specialist

**Status:** `playtest` provisional (2026-10-11).  
**Primary lore:** Blizzard [LotV Adept introduction](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/18291111/legacy-of-the-void-beta-preview) describes **ranged attack** and Psionic Transfer/Shade positioning; role corroborated by officially licensed TMG Adept Glaive Cannon and shade card, **not a numerical weapon range source**.

**Adept (Extra; Size0 Normal Scale)**  
**Attributes:** Agility **d10**, Smarts **d8**, Spirit **d8**, Strength **d8**, Vigor **d8**.  
**Skills:** Athletics **d10**, Fighting **d6**, Notice **d8**, Shooting **d8**, Stealth **d8**, Psionics **—** unless trained.  
**Pace 7**, **Parry 5**, **Toughness 14 (4 Armor, +4 shield Toughness)** / **10 (4) shield off**.

**Gear and abilities**
- **Adept light harness:** **+4 ordinary Armor** and a separate **SFC p.42 Personal Force Field** equivalent as Candidate A, no Heavy Armor/Class by default.
- **Glaive Cannon:** **12/24/48**, **2d10/AP2**, **RoF1**, **Heavy Weapon/Class I** (avg11+AP2=13), **Shooting-based**; these numbers are **original RPG conversions**. Distinct from TMG RNG8 and SC2 range. Against Marine ClassI CMC it can cause ClassI Wounds; against Marauder II normally only on Shooting raise.
- **Psionic Transfer (optional specialized suit talent):** For **first pass, use Core Teleport (printed p.170)** only when **appropriate specialized psi-equipment or Arcane Background** is present; **2 PP**, Seasoned and arcane skill, up to 12\" or 24\" on a raise; unseen −2. **Not every ordinary Adept Extra automatically knows teleport** for free. If an NPC's Shade is specifically needed in a scenario, give it a **prepaid 1-use device** activating the Teleport power with a visible/attackable telegraph marker as an original variant, not a free teleport every turn.
- **Telepathy:** Baseline SFC ancestry, separate from all combat powers.

**Role:** mobile light-infantry duelist and flanker, not Zealot with another sword or Stalker-style heavy armor sniper. Test equipment-based Teleport versus PP-trained Adept variant in scenarios with high terrain and C-14 fire.
