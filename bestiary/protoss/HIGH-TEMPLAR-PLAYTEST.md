# High Templar — Psionic Support Specialist

**Status:** `playtest`, revised for Core/SFC powers (2026-10-11).  
**Blizzard:** [1998 High Templar](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/units/templar.shtml) explicitly calls these **trained powerful psionic** battlefield support and names Storm / Hallucination / Archon merge. **Not every Protoss** has these abilities.

**High Templar (Veteran Wild Card, Size0 Normal Scale)**  
**Attributes:** Agility **d6**, Smarts **d12**, Spirit **d12**, Strength **d6**, Vigor **d8**.  
**Skills:** Academics **d10**, Battle **d10**, Fighting **d4**, Notice **d10**, Occult **d10**, Psionics **d12**, Persuasion **d8**, Stealth **d4**.  
**Pace6; Parry4; Toughness 14 (4 physical Armor, +4 field Toughness)** / **10 (4) without field**.  
**Edges:** **Arcane Background (Psionics), SFC Psyker variant p.201**; published requirements satisfied, **Power Points 10**, three chosen starting powers.

**Three sample Powers / trappings** (Core rules and SFC available-list; choose only three, not entire campaign spell list):
1. **Psionic Storm:** **Blast** (Core printed p.156): **3 PP, Range Smarts×2, Medium Blast Template, 2d6 damage (3d6 on arcane raise)**, or published +2 PP for 3d6 (4d6 raise), using Psionics d12. **Additional prototype StarCraft adaptation:** to have lore-grounded effect through **CMC Heavy Armor**, mark the Storm as **Heavy Weapon Class I**; it then deals Heavy Metal ClassI direct Wounds versus applicable ClassI armor. **This HW classification is CUSTOM, not part of Core Blast RAW**. If that is too lethal over a template, instead consider arcane effect ignoring conventional armor without Heavy Metal; do not use both simultaneously.
2. **Hallucination:** **Illusion** (Core power), appropriate decoys at ordinary published PP/casting/counter-detection requirements. Hallucinated units don't deal genuine melee wounds and do not acquire every physical ability of the form they copy.
3. **Psionic Defense:** **Deflection** (Core power), standard duration and PP, with a psychic ward Trapping. A protective illusion or defense power is **distinct** from the personal physical **shield emitter**'s +4 Toughness. Apply normal armor/power stacking constraints.

**Innate** short-range Telepathy (SFC ancestry) remains available without spending PPs, not a global Khala mind-network command ability.  
**No free Archon merge:** the Archon is a major entity/ritual requiring its own scene and encounter profile; do not treat two Extras merging as a free combat action granting huge HP pools.

**Threat and risk:** Ordinary personal C-14 still hits conventional Toughness rather than class; Storm classed explosion against multiple Marines could be very powerful, so use limited PP, action costs and positioning. Core Blast already has a damage/area rule; the extra Heavy Weapon designation is a deliberate StarCraft exception pending owner choice.
