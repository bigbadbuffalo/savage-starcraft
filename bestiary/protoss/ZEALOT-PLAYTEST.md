# Zealot — Melee Psionic Shock Infantry

**Status:** `playtest` numbers (2026-10-11), pending shield choice, armor modeling and owner approval.  
**Primary lore:** [Blizzard's Zealot guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/units/zealot.shtml): **power suits channel innate psi energy into shields and forearm energy blades**; warriors can cut armored structures and vehicles. [Remastered spotlight](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/20898713/unit-spotlight-protoss) verifies melee vulnerability during approach. Licensed TMG Zealot cards treat **Armour** and **Shield** as separate statistics; no direct numbers adopted.

**Zealot (seasoned Extra / leader can be Wild Card, Size0 Normal Scale)**  
**Attributes:** Agility **d8**, Smarts **d8**, Spirit **d10**, Strength **d10 natural / d12+1 with active matched suit** (**custom suit assistance**), Vigor **d10**.  
**Skills:** Athletics **d10**, Fighting **d10**, Intimidation **d8**, Notice **d8**, Stealth **d6**, Psionics **—** unless specially trained.  
**Pace 6**, **Parry 7**, **Toughness 17 (6 armor, +4 personal field Toughness)** when shield on; **Toughness 13 (6)** when shield down. Base Vigor d10 gives base Toughness **7**, plus ordinary worn +6 Armor, +4 field Toughness. **Do NOT list Armor +10 or Heavy Metal Class**.

**Gear / Special Abilities**
- **Zealot Combat Power Harness:** **+6 *ordinary* Armor** (SFC p.42 Infantry Battle Suit structural analogue, **not** literal CMC/Power Armor Frames). **Suit-assisted Strength d12+1** is an original equipment test statistic reflecting lore power assistance, not a published automatic effect of SFC p.42 infantry battle armor. The suit and shield can be separately disabled. No unlimited paid Power Armor Mod capacity inferred.
- **Personal Plasma Field:** Initially use **SFC p.42 Personal Force Field** (+4 Toughness, stacks with worn Armor, Size2 or smaller and published 3-Wound overload). **Candidate A only**; full depletion and recharge remains unresolved; no simultaneous built-in Candidate B.
- **Twin Psi-Blades:** **Str+d10, AP8, HW Class II** with **suited Str d12+1** (average7.5+5.5+8 = **21**, threshold Class II). **One Fighting attack using one blade by default**; 2 forearm projections do **not** grant a second free attack. Optional **Two-Fisted** only for trained champion (and follow actual offhand/multiple-action rules). This is a **deliberate more powerful conversion than published SFC Psi Blade Str+d8/AP4/ClassI**, selected as *candidate* based on Blizzard vehicle-cutting fiction.
- **Innate communication:** Use **SFC p.8 Telepathy** (1 ancestry point) mind-to-mind with sapients within **12\"**. Doesn't confer Psionics AB/powers, doesn't recreate the pre-LotV Khala after its severance.
- **Charge:** A mobile shock-infantry unit; use regular Athletics, Running, Hold, Defend, Gang Up/First Strike and enemy cover rules. *Zealot Charge +2 free* is **not** granted because TMG/RTS have a charge animation. A later leg-enhancement suit Upgrade may increase Pace, costing an actual equipment option.

**Combat role:** Zealot can menace **Class I Marines (1d3+1 direct Wounds)** and **Class II Marauders (1d3)** when it reaches melee; no all-range psi attacks by default. C-14 vs this Zealot resolves **ordinary rolled damage** (physical suit Armor6 minus C-14 AP4, and +4 shield Toughness) rather than Class I direct Wounds. Elite ability/Field recharge need owner consideration.

**Important:** A literal heavy-armor SFC suit would change every incoming Class interaction dramatically; the chosen defensive mechanic is an **unclassed suit approximation** despite Blizzard calling it powered. Re-evaluate with other factions before declaring the suit type final.
