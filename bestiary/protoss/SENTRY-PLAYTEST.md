# Sentry — Robotic Barrier and Defensive Field Platform

**Status:** `playtest` device concept; specific technology and action cost remain draft (2026-10-11).  
**Primary:** Blizzard [Force Field](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/5963109/game-guide-protoss-force-field) physically blocks transit through a chokepoint; [Hallucination](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/5963110/game-guide-protoss-hallucination) creates non-damaging decoy units; Guardian Shield protects nearby allies from ranged fire. Officially licensed [TMG Protoss Sentry](https://starcraft-tmg.com/files/downloads/StarCraft-Protoss-P2P-Card-Sheets-A4_EN.pdf) separately provides Guardian Shield, placed Force Field and hallucination. **Sentry is a purpose-built support construct, not a High Templar or automatically endowed with natural Protoss Telepathy.**

**Sentry (Construct Extra, Size0 Normal Scale)**  
**Attributes:** Agility **d6**, Smarts **d8**, Spirit **d8**, Strength **d6**, Vigor **d8**.  
**Skills:** Electronics **d10**, Notice **d10**, Shooting **d6**, Repair **d6**, Stealth **d6**, Fighting **d4**.  
**Pace6; Parry4; Toughness14 (4 ordinary Armor, +4 personal field Toughness)**, or **10(4)** shield off. **Core Construct** traits, printed p.176; Repairs replace healing.

**Installed systems — first usable rules options**
- **Disruption Beam:** **8/16/32, 2d6/AP2, RoF1, ordinary non-HW**; ranged Shooting. No Heavy Metal Class. Primarily support weapon, can't defeat Class II suits just by targeting them.
- **Barrier Projector:** Activation by **Electronics** or **Weird Science** skill based on device configuration, using Core **Barrier** printed p.155: **2 PP**, Seasoned requirement if a caster PC, 5\" long/1\" tall line wall, Hardness10 (12 on raise), **duration 5 rounds**. A standalone sentry device must have an **identified 10-point device power reserve** (NPC test pool) and one action per ordinary activated power; it does not get unlimited instant walls just because a game hotkey has no cooldown. **This is a device analogy, not an actual published Sentry gadget**.
- **Guardian Shield:** **Core Deflection**-based programmed ally defense (2 PP base, Duration5, -2 to ranged attacks; recipients bought by printed power modifier). **Not** the same as personal field Toughness or an automatic +2 Armor aura; add recipients with PP cost. An alternate **Protection** Trapping can be used if a GM prefers absorbing damage to deflecting fire, but choose one per device.
- **Hallucination:** Use published **Illusion** power via appropriate Weird Science-style emitter (paid PP and action). Decoys are not full-strength Zealot constructs, and no default duplicate wielded weapons inflict real Wounds. Counter sensors/Notice follow the actual power.

**Power architecture caveat:** Core Arcane Background (Weird Science) or suitable SFC devices supply rules for activating technical powers; **an Extra Sentry does not receive a universal PC-level 3 powers/10 PP for no cost** in actual PC construction. This entry's **10 point power supply is a provisional encounter budget** for its three technical devices. GM can provide it as NPC gear. Track the PP/action economy for Sentry support in the integrated test and separately budget devices for PCs.

**Combat use:** a Sentry with a few PP can stop Zerglings at a passage or buy Zealots approach time under C-14 fire, without simply turning every ally permanently invulnerable. Massive foes may be able to destroy or step over barrier according to actual Hardness/size of wall, **not** automatic RTS "Massive" immunity mapped to SWADE Size.
