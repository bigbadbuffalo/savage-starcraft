# Protoss — First Ground Role Profiles (2026-10-11)

**Status:** `playtest` equipment/NPC prototypes, **not approved final species or shield design**. The Protoss' mixed roster makes them more than a melee-only race: **Zealots** close with blades, **Adepts** fire ranged glaive energy, **Stalkers** provide armored-target fire, **Sentries** are specialized robotic support, **High Templar** are psionic casters, **Dark Templar** are stealth blade specialists. **Dragoons** are living Protoss connected to walkers and will need separate vehicle-scale work.

**Baseline test shield:** `Candidate A` from [Protoss Shield Systems](../../equipment/protoss/SHIELD-SYSTEMS-PLAYTEST.md): SFC **p.42 Personal Force Field** +4 Toughness, stacks with worn Armor, Size2 wearer limit, battery fails on 3+ Wounds in one hit. **This is a FIRST CONVERSION ALTERNATIVE, not a user-approved canon-equivalent shield subsystem.** Also research `Candidate B`, SFC p.123 **charged Soak Shield Mod**, for true depletion/recharge and interaction with Classed Heavy Metal armor. Never stack A+B as ordinary issue by implication.

**Size0** for Protoss humanoids is an initial SWADE **Normal Scale choice**, **not** a claim that every Protoss is human height. SFC Personal Field covers Size<=2. Do not equate visual stature, RTS target tag or TMG Size.

**Interfaction tension:** Against `ordinary` +6 Armor Zealot, C-14 rolls **2d10/AP4** vs Vigor Toughness plus Armor/shield; against **Class II mechanical Stalker**, C-14 Class I normally cannot inflict Heavy Metal Wounds except on a raise. The +4 Toughness personal field does **not** mitigate such Class Wounds, so a **charge-based shield** is a testable candidate for armored platforms. Do not assign fixed Class to a Zealot solely because Blizzard calls it a "power suit."
