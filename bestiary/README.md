# Bestiary and NPC Profiles

**State:** **First provisional Zerg ground bestiary is available for playtest design; no individual creature stats are final-approved**. Terran and Protoss NPCs are still being built.

Provide ready-to-run profiles even where the underlying character options are modular. Creature/Extra/NPC statistics do not need to satisfy PC ancestry or advancement point budgets.

Initial test group: Terran infantryman and technician, Zergling, Hydralisk, one larger Zerg threat, and a Protoss combatant after psionics/shields are scoped.

Each entry should name variant and era, summarize sourced behavior, and distinguish profile statistics from detachable equipment.

## First organism baseline (2026-10-11)
The owner selected a **morphology/physical-scale analogue first** method, then a broad sweep across even unexpected SWADE Companions for behavior, ecology, and supernatural-seeming bioabilities. Six usable first-pass Zerg organisms are indexed at [Zerg ground bestiary](zerg/README.md): **Zergling, Hydralisk, Roach, Baneling, Lurker, Ultralisk**, plus [Creep and infestation hazards](zerg/CREEP-AND-SWARM-HABITAT.md). Detailed cross-Companion source locators at [Zerg mechanics survey](../development/benchmarks/ZERG-SWADE-CREATURE-AND-MECHANICS-SURVEY.md); future test scenarios at [Integrated Zerg checklist](../development/benchmarks/ZERG-INTEGRATED-PLAYTEST-CHECKLIST.md).

The initial Ultralisk **Class III Heavy Armor** is an **exceptional, unapproved creature conversion**. Ordinary Zerg natural shells still are **not classed Heavy Armor**; the small Zergling's **Class I Heavy Weapon scythes** are a separate attack tag to permit challenging Class I Marines. Neither choice is a universal Zerg rule.
