# StarCraft: Tabletop Miniatures Game (2026) — Cross-System Source Review

**Review date:** 2026-10-10. **Evidence type:** Licensed derivative tabletop game, **not** a statement of canonical real-world weapon dimensions.  
**Source ID:** `SC-TMG-2026` (see `lore/SOURCE-REGISTRY.md`).  
**Status:** Initial comparative review, not exhaustive. The *Command Center* landing page publicly displays an **Enter as Guest** affordance and **Factions & Cards** navigation, but we could not actually operate its dynamic card browser in the available web-access environment. Accordingly, **no unit numbers below are attributed to private/app-only cards**; every quoted number is from *public printable PDFs*.
 
## Licensing and release
Archon Studio FAQ https://starcraft-tmg.com/faq explicitly confirms official Blizzard licensing. This is an **officially licensed adaptation**, not a fan wargame. Its official source status **does not** make any of its attack probabilities, balance prices or inch ranges diegetic facts. Official FAQ states the miniatures use **32 mm scale with adjustments**, battlefield **36\"×36\" (small)** and **54\"×36\" (normal)**, and balance is **mathematical with living adjustments**.

Accessible source list: https://starcraft-tmg.com/downloads ; user-supplied full rules https://starcraft-tmg.com/files/downloads/StarCraft-TMG_EN.pdf (large PDF; direct full in-tool display size-limited); rules overview https://starcraft-tmg.com/news/rules/starcraft-tabletop-miniatures-game-rules ; quick-start PDF https://starcraft-tmg.com/files/downloads/StarCraft-TMG-Quick-Start-Rules_EN.pdf (see **PDF p. 4** for weapon card schema); public unit-card sheets below.

## What the printed game stats **mean**
**Quick Start Rules**, PDF p. 3: all measurements are **horizontal base-to-base tabletop inches**; p. 4 defines **RNG** as *maximum tabletop weapon targeting distance*, **RoA** as dice per model, and **Surge Type** as the enemy type that activates weapon Surge. Armor is a D6 **success target for blocking hits**; not SWADE Armor points or Heavy Metal Class. **Shield** is a separate HP buffer for first model and the quick-start says it **cannot be healed back**. Do **not** read this as canon that Protoss shields cannot recharge.

**The printable unit sheets carry the concrete data; the long core rulebook explains how it functions.**

## Other useful patterns
1. **Terran specialist loadouts:** Marine's card contains a plausible wargame mechanism for single-model AGG-12/rocket-launcher specialists, directly relevant to the Savage StarCraft plan for mounted/heavy weapons support. But names/card abilities are **licensed game interpretations, not proof of continuity**.
2. **Heavy infantry differentiation:** Marines tagged **Light**; Marauders tagged **Armoured** and their Quad K12 gets **Armoured** surge and PIERCE. This supports comparing target roles and combined arms, **not a numerical SWADE Class translation**.
3. **Medical and defensive support:** The Medic card has **Medpack, Life Support, Restoration, Optical Flare** and the A-13 grenade launcher as an upgrade. Useful *ability-category checklist* for SWADE, not a reason to duplicate command point mechanics.
4. **Protoss shield model differs:** The quick-start Shield is **one-time added HP, not rechargeable**. This is a particularly clear warning against treating licensed wargame rules as canon Protoss technology.
5. **Rules change:** [Changelog of 9 October 2026](https://starcraft-tmg.com/files/downloads/StarCraft-TMG-Changelog-10.2026_EN.pdf) increases costs for Marines/Marauders, reduces Zergling costs, and discusses improving competitive roster viability. This is strong **direct evidence of active balance tuning**.

## What to adopt in Savage StarCraft
- **Adopt as source / comparator:** Unit roles, targeting differences (Light vs Armoured), specialist loadout possibilities, alternative Shield/medic/ability design examples, tabletop range *ordering* only when supported by multiple sources.
- **Do not adopt as canon or convert literally:** RNG inches, hit/armor probabilities, per-model HP, Surge dice, RoA, energy/resource costs, shield recharge behavior, 32 mm miniature dimensions, and army supply point economics.
- **Retain existing provisional C-14** SWADE Range **30/60/120**. TMG supplies no canonical range in actual meters or yards, so does **not** validate or refute that range. No numerical change to the user's provisional baseline.
- **Later research opportunity:** With browser interaction, try the Command Center **Enter as Guest → Factions & Cards** for the current live card version; note any departure from publicly downloadable 2026 v1.0 sheets. Never claim app-only stats were read without actually accessing them.

## Authority caveat
Archon Studio is officially licensed by Blizzard, but its **competitive miniatures rules are adaptations**, just as older licensed RPG rule mechanics are not automatically canonical.
