# Protoss Natural Psionics Versus Trained Disciplines — Initial RPG Design

**Date:** 2026-10-11. **Status:** `draft/playtest` conceptual ancestry/advancement distinction. **Not an owner-approved Protoss player ancestry** or blanket bonus.

## Fictional distinctions
Blizzard [1998 Zealot guide](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/units/zealot.shtml): warriors have innate psionic capacity, channeled by training and suit tech. [High Templar](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/units/templar.shtml): unusually potent trained war mystics. [Dark Templar](https://classic.battle.net/scc/protoss/units/darktemplar.shtml): shadow/void arts. [Development Q&A 13](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/8501031/starcraft-ii-creative-development-q-a-part-13) describes worker Khalai, Templar military, and Judicator governance; not one identical training profile. [LotV Story Primer](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23331587/starcraft-story-primer) and [post-Khala Selendis short story](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23485205/short-story-one-people-one-purpose) distinguish the old **Khala gestalt** from continuing individual Protoss consciousness after the severing.

## Three conceptual layers

| Layer | Possible SWADE expression | Who gets it? | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Species-wide communication / sensitivity** | **SFC Ancestral Telepathy**, printed p.8, **1 design point**, communicates mind-to-mind within **12\"/24yd** regardless of language; optional sense emotional intent as narrative flavor, no passive mind reading | **Provisional ancestry feature** for basic Protoss NPCs/PCs; post-Khala **not** a hivewide long-range universal broadcast | **Does not confer** three Powers, a Psionics skill die, or a Psionics Arcane Background |
| **Focused trained channeling / psionic tools** | Suit/weapon **psi-blades**, standard Fighting attacks; published **Psychic weapon quality** on SFC pp.46,50 provides optional spend **1–3 Power Points = +2 rolled damage per PP** for wielders with Psionics AB | Zealots, combat Templar, Nerazim, users of appropriate gear | A basic Zealot may use a powered psi-blade **without getting free full High Templar caster list**; reserve bonus PP channeling for PC/specialist who actually pays and knows how |
| **Advanced psionic feats** | SFC **Psyker Edge** printed **p.201**, requirements **Smarts d6+**, **Psionics (Smarts)** skill, **3 starting Powers / 10 PP**; Core Powers **blast, illusion, deflection, barrier, puppet, havoc, etc.** | High Templar, named Judicators, specialized advanced Dark Templar, exceptional individuals of other castes | **Explicit specialization/advancement**, not baseline all Protoss. Retain PP, activation rolls, disruption/counterplay and player build budgets |

## Khala, Nerazim and era
- In pre-*Legacy of the Void* Aiur societies, the **Khala** is a historical mass empathic/telepathic network and cultural social structure, not just the Core 12\" Telepathy Ancestral Ability, **not** a blanket +4 Psionics combat bonus.
- In/after *Legacy of the Void*, Aiur Protoss **sever the Khala bond** to deny Amon access; individual psionic talent **continues**. No "all Protoss lose telepathy" automatic downgrade without fiction support.
- **Nerazim** practice void disciplines without Khala compliance; a generic Psychic blade may model base technology, while trained dark Templar use **SFC Cloaking Skin/Invisibility** or a specialized power with actual upkeep. Do not conflate void concealment with a Khala feature.
- **Robotics:** Dragoons may be piloted by Protoss minds; Stalkers and Sentries have specialized platforms/technical drives. A robot model does not automatically gain ancestral telepathy as a *species trait*. Instead its psi communications and interface come from installed systems.

## NPC vs player use
**At table:** Ordinary Khalai envoy or technician receives plausible telepathic communication without carrying psionic combat ammunition. A Zealot Extra uses Fighting and its suit-supported psi blades without tracking 10 generic Power Points (unless its specialty needs paid bursts). A High Templar Wild Card uses Psionics skill, 10 PP and three selected powers as printed by the SFC Psyker Edge; don't give all SC1/SC2 game spells plus a permanent free shield for zero budget cost. **PC balancing** must follow final ancestry budget and selected Edges.

**Outlook:** After three-faction testing, develop an ancestry point table with genuine tradeoffs (strength, telepathy, sensitivity, long life, handicaps) and choose appropriate role-specific psionic Edge tracks. Don't replace every Protoss warrior with a rigid character class.
