# Savage StarCraft — Setting Rules baseline v0.1
**Approved by project owner, 2026-10-10.** This approves the small rule package, **not** any particular CMC Armor stat or an unresolved Heavy Metal/character combat interface.

| Rule | Published source | Project scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More Skill Points | SWADE Core p. 140 | Default starting characters: 15 Skill Points |
| Heavy Metal | Sci-Fi Companion p. 93 | Preferred vehicle/heavy machinery damage; infantry power-armor interface needs decision |
| Giant Foes | Sci-Fi Companion p. 78 | Normal situational option against qualifying exceptionally large foes |
| Extreme Environments | Sci-Fi Companion pp. 82–83 | Situational GM rules for hostile atmospheres, gravity, pressure and vacuum |

**Campaign-only options:** Logistics, Energy Management, Downtime, Hacking, Salvage & Trade, Clashes, tactical vehicle combat, Gritty Damage, Difficult Healing and Horror Companion expanded Fear consequences. Hard Choices, Villainous Conviction and Super Powers Combined Attacks are **not** general defaults.

**Heavy Metal caveat:** SFC p. 93 describes vehicle-class direct Wounds; p. 120 supplies Classes to power armor but p. 121 also uses *wearer's Wounds, Soak and suit critical hits*. An unqualified application of the class table to individuals can produce abrupt armor-threshold lethality. Until its proper scope is deliberately selected, **first tests use ordinary SWADE character damage for power-armored individuals and Heavy Metal for vehicles/walkers/starships**. This is a *proposed testing interpretation*, not a final published-RAW claim or approved house rule. See `development/benchmarks/HEAVY-METAL-INTERFACE.md`.
