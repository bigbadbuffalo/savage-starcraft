# AAV-5 Arclite Siege Tank — Mobile Armor & Deployed Artillery

**Status:** `playtest` source-matched vehicle prototype, 2026-10-11; siege-transform actions, actual ammo and SWADE Class discrepancy remain **draft**.  
**Blizzard primary:** https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/ut.shtml identifies **AAV-5 Arclite**, **three-person crew (driver, gunner, commander/navigator)**, **mobile combat tank**, and **immobile braced Siege Mode** for extremely powerful **Mjolnir Artillery Cannon**; siege fire has a minimum-effective range and risks striking nearby friendly units. Original gameplay damage/range are not physical artillery figures.  
**SWADE chassis analogue:** SFC **Main Battle Tank**, printed **p.174**, **Size8 Huge, Handling−2, Top Speed4, Toughness42(22), ClassIV, 5 Wounds**, tracked, and **Heavy Cannon** plus lighter secondary fire. This is a far more suitable benchmark than scaling Marine HP to vehicle Toughness.

## Basic chassis
**Arclite AAV-5:** **Size8 Huge**, **Toughness42(22) / ClassIV**, **5 vehicle Wounds**, **Handling−2**, **Top Speed Rating4**, tracked, **crew3**, **Sealed if fitted**, 3-person sighting/command fire-control. Size, mass, price and speed are **SFC RPG estimates**, not actual Arclite model specifications.

## Two firing modes — specific proto-stats
- **Mobile/Tank Mode:** chassis may move and use **SFC Medium Cannon**, p.59: **Range75/150/300, 4d10/AP10, ClassIII, RoF1, Small Blast Template** armor-piercing attack. HE variant **4d8/AP5, Large Blast Template, ClassII**. Actual Arclite weapons may use different projectiles; this is a flexible conversion.
- **Deployed/Siege Mode:** immobilize/braced artillery chassis; proposed **SFC Heavy Cannon**, p.59: **Range100/200/400, 5d10/AP20, Heavy Weapon**, **Small Blast Template**, optional HE **5d8/AP10 LBT**. **Important published-book discrepancy:** SFC lists its **Heavy Cannon as ClassIV**, but p.93 Class formula from **avg 27.5 + AP20 = 47.5** would put that AP shell in **Class V** (46–55). **Do not silently choose a Class**: for the first package, **use printed weapon label IV as provisional** pending an official correction/errata review, and track formula-based V as test alternative. The printed Heavy Cannon's **HE LBT uses ClassIII**. This is **SFC internal inconsistency**, not StarCraft model lore.
- **Mode change:** transformation requires the tank to be **stationary** and spend a **full action** deploying or retracting; **custom SWADE action-cost choice**, not Blizzard's actual mechanical seconds. No movement in Siege Mode. **Close dead zone:** use a **GM-chosen minimum 6\" range** for the siege cannon as an original encounter constraint; do not mistake original RTS tile-distance for yards. A direct-hit close assault may force redeployment.
- **Friendly fire:** SBT/LBT follows normal area-effect targeting; in heavy HE siege bombardment, nearby allies inside the template are not automatically protected. Real blast cover/Evasion and artillery spotting matter.

**Actual vehicle system:** Separate three-person crew may have actions, injuries and appropriate Crew Critical Hits under Core rules; track **vehicle Wounds, vehicle criticals, crew protection, sealed breaches, Out of Control for attempted maneuvering**, and repairs by mechanics. Unlike an Ultralisk, the tank is *really* a vehicle and cannot heal flesh.

**Weapon and Class ecology:** Marine C14 ClassI cannot hurt ClassIV hardened tank; current **ClassIII rocket** versus IV normally 0 Wounds, 1 on attack raise; **siege cannon ClassIV as printed** versus **Ultralisk ClassIII** yields 1d3+1 Wounds (plus raise). Tank anti-vehicle fire is battlefield decisive, but not a routine personal rifle equivalent. Full ammo counts and turret weapon mount/Mods remain later construction questions.
