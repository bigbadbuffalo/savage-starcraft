# Terran Vulture — Fast Scout Hover Bike (first vehicle conversion)

**Status:** `playtest` candidate, 2026-10-11; gear availability, mine and weapon profile not owner-approved.  
**Blizzard primary:** https://classic.battle.net/scc/terran/uv.shtml identifies **Vulture hover bike**, fast scout/patrol craft, **anti-personnel grenade launcher**, optional **Spider Mines**; quotes approximately **230mph** (this is official unit technical prose, unlike tactical DPS). Its game upgrade limits are **not physical inventory laws**.  
**Published source analogue:** SFC **Combat Hover Cycle**, printed **p.173** (Size2, Handling+2, Top Speed Rating11 ≈200mph, Toughness10(2), **ClassI**, 2 vehicle Wounds, crew1, hover propulsion), rather than using StarCraft's RTS "Medium" size directly.

## Base chassis
**Vulture scout cycle:** **Size2 Normal**, **Handling+2**, **Top Speed11** (SFC speed convention), **Toughness10 (2)**, **Vehicle Class I**, **2 vehicle Wounds**, **Crew1, exposed**, Hover. **Published analogue values**, not a canon exact Vulture mass or perfect 230mph speed. Optional superior Ion Thrusters upgrade may improve Top Speed per SFC power/fuel Mods later.

## Attached weapon and mines (distinct explicit adaptation)
- **Anti-personnel grenade launcher:** Treat as **SFC vehicular grenade launcher** printed p.60, **Range24/48/96, Damage3d6/AP0 Heavy Weapon ClassI, MBT**, **12 shots**, minimum mount Size1. **RoF1** for this first Vulture conversion is a **custom anti-personnel single-launch firing mode** rather than the stock vehicle grenade launcher RoF3. Potential SFC **Beehive** Cone shells can reflect close suppressive fire without copying StarCraft RTS Concussive damage modifiers. No K12-only AP Punisher compatibility applies to this generic gun.
- **Spider Mines** (optional): Adapt the published **SFC Mine** printed p.62 **4d6/AP10 Heavy Weapon ClassII, Large Blast Template, one use**, with sensors and optional active pursuit as **not-yet-costed StarCraft customization**. Blizzard guide says a Vulture can deploy **3** game mines after an upgrade, but the RPG can limit mine load by actual cargo capacity and mission procurement rather than claiming every mined Vulture universally has 3.
- Trigger/detection: buried hidden mines need **Notice/Scanner** and appropriate camo, possible Electronics/Repair/Thievery disarm, and an **approach/movement step** for autonomous spider mines, **not** immediate unavoidable detonation at arbitrary range. This is **a draft tactical sequence**, not a finished Spider Mine AI rule.

**Role & interactions:** It is an actual vehicle with rider/pilot; use **vehicle Wounds, Vehicle Critical Hits, Driving**, exposed operator risks and appropriate Out-of-Control/maneuvering procedures. C14 ClassI can hit ClassI Vulture **1d3 Wounds** before applicable vehicle procedures, so it is not a substitute for a tank. Compare pursuit with Zergling Pace10 and scanner hazards.
