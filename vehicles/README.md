# Vehicles, Walkers and Starships

**State:** First three provisional Terran vehicle conversions and a biological giant-monster scaling survey exist; no final-approved vehicle stats.

Use SWADE Vehicle Guide as a **conversion reference**, and the Science Fiction Companion for mechanics appropriate to ground vehicles, walkers, starfighters and capital ships. Scale, target profile, mass, armor, and tactical role are separately established; dimensions are evidence, not an automatic damage formula.

Do not normalize battlecruisers to targets vulnerable to ordinary small arms for the sake of RTS unit balance.

Start only after infantry armor/AP benchmarks are documented.

## First ground and biological-scale anchors (2026-10-11)
- [Vulture hover bike](terran/VULTURE-HOVER-BIKE-PLAYTEST.md): SFC Combat Hover Cycle, **Size2/T10(2)/ClassI**, patrol cannon and optional Spider Mines.
- [Goliath combat walker](terran/GOLIATH-PLAYTEST.md): SFC Infantry Support Walker, **Size4/T21(6)/ClassII**, paired light autocannons, anti-air light missiles.
- [AAV-5 Arclite Siege Tank](terran/ARCLITE-SIEGE-TANK-PLAYTEST.md): SFC Main Battle Tank, **Size8/T42(22)/ClassIV**, distinct mobile/siege weapon systems. **SFC p.59 heavy cannon's printed ClassIV differs from its p.93 formula ClassV**, flagged pending adjudication.
- [Biological Vehicle Scale Study](../development/benchmarks/BIOLOGICAL-VEHICLE-SCALE-STUDY.md): *Vehicle Guide* p.21 **'Monsters as Vehicles'** is a conversion aid, not a mandate to use complete vehicle mechanics on organisms. Current Ultralisk **T26(10)/ClassIII** derived normally; enormous Nydus/Overlord/Leviathan forms may later use specialized biological vehicle rules.
